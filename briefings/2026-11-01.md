## One Thing Worth Your Attention Today
The shared Google Search grounding limits represent a hidden cost structure that affects model selection beyond per-token pricing: the 500 RPD free limit shared between Gemini 2.5 Flash and Flash-Lite, and the 5K free monthly pool shared across all Gemini 3+ models (Argon, Flash 3.8, Live, Robotics, TTS), mean that any organization deploying Retrieval-Augmented Generation workloads must explicitly model grounding consumption across model tiers. Before committing to a Gemini model tier for production RAG, validate the expected grounding request volume against the shared free quotas and model-specific limits, or budget the per-request overage cost into your infrastructure runway.
