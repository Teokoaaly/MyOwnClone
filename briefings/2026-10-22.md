## The Three Things Worth Your Attention

### 1. The `/admin` code leak is the sharpest finding

A GET to `/admin` with no session returns **HTTP 200 and 266 KB of HTML** containing the admin panel's RSC payload — before any redirect. The auth barrier is applied client-side via `<meta refresh>`, not server-side. 81+ internal feature references download unauthenticated, including the impersonation module and the full subscription-state vocabulary.

The fix is moving the barrier to the server. That is an afternoon of work, not a redesign.

Caveat: the June scrape logged a 307 redirect on the same route. Two observations, two dates. **Re-verify with curl before you act on it** — the atom is marked `solid` because the May report states it as directly confirmed, but the conflict is real and unresolved.
