## The Four Things Worth Your Attention

### 1. The cost model has two holes in it

The vault's cost model splits spend into "tenant-billable inference" and "platform-absorbed ops" ([[2026-1022-costes-por-categoria-tenant]]). Neither bucket has a line for **hosting** and neither has a line for **per-signup verification**.

Hetzner's cheapest viable shared plan is **CPX02, 1 vCPU, €5.99/month** (€0.0096/h). The vault's stated budget is **≤ €4/month**. No plan in the catalog fits. That is a decision, not a research question.

And the panel's "por proveedor" breakdown only measures API-by-token. A flat subscription — ChatGPT Plus, an Alibaba Coding Plan at $50, OpenCode Go at $10/mo — is a different unit the panel structurally cannot see.

### 2. The verification market is documented, and it is not a good look

5sim, HeroSMS, lzt.market and PriceAI's 卡网 table were atomized in full: 1221 catalogued services, per-country CSV prices, delivery rates, the 10 % top-up commission, and the regional subscription arbitrage (ChatGPT Go Egypt −56 %, Gemini Ultra 5× Turkey −70 %).

The vault **records** this market and does not endorse it. Three reasons are on the record: OpenAI blocks VoIP numbers, SMS-Activate retired its own name as a scam marker, and the price claims contradict the measurements three different ways.

**It is adjacent, not in the product.** The live `/registro` uses magic link + 6-digit code + Google OAuth — no phone step at all. If that is deliberate, none of this is a roadmap item. If it is accidental, it comes with ToS exposure attached.

### 3. The inference layer is one environment variable wide

MyOwnClone points at a single provider via `OPENAI_API_BASE` (DeepSeek by default). The new-api atoms document what a real gateway layer gives you: channel groups, ratio-based pricing, calibrated token counting, failover, per-group margins — and what it costs: AGPLv3 attribution, Postgres + Redis next to a stateless nginx, and conversion loss in the protocol shim.

The relay market it sits in prices at **0.01×–7.00×** official API, with sponsored placement inside the same table as the ranking. The midjourney-proxy atoms are the honest archetype: when there is no official API, someone builds an unofficial bridge — the same structural gap as our own `myownclone_public_bp` chat endpoint, still 404.

### 4. The security field has a taxonomy, and only some of it applies

The curated "Awesome AI Security Tools" list was atomized as a 15-category map. For a multi-tenant AI SaaS, the live categories are runtime protection, guardrails/red-teaming, MCP as attack surface, telemetry and agent security. Model-weight scanning, SAST-for-AI and CTF material are not yet relevant — and the list itself warns it is not a scanner.

Three frictions came out of it: the field routinely qualifies agent runtimes as **not** providing complete isolation (our entity note claims "aislamiento completo entre tenants"); telemetry-on-by-default is now normal, which reframes our public Sentry DSN; and the AI-triage layer presupposes observability we do not have.
