## Claim

Incogniton's cookie policy classifies cookies into three categories: **Functional** cookies are always active and strictly necessary for site functionality; **Statistics** cookies collect anonymous statistical data for site usage analysis; **Marketing** cookies create user profiles to send advertising or track users across websites for similar marketing purposes.

## Why It Matters

The three-tier cookie classification mirrors the pricing tier structure: Functional cookies are available on all plans, Statistics cookies support analytics that help Incogniton optimize its service offerings, and Marketing cookies are the basis for targeted advertising that may influence plan conversion rates. Users who reject Marketing cookies may experience reduced personalization in the pricing page experience, though core functionality remains intact.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-incogniton-precios]] describe cuatro planes de precios con diferentes conjuntos de características. Esta nota presiona esto porque la disponibilidad de funcionalidades como **Cookie Collector** y **Profile Synchronizer** varía por plan, y los usuarios que rechazarán cookies de marketing pueden tener acceso limitado a características de seguimiento que están ligadas a la optimización de conversión de planes. Ambas no pueden ser completamente correctas sin determinar si la restricción de cookies afecta directamente a la funcionalidad de los planes o solo a la experiencia de marketing.
