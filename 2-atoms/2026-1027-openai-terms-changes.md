## Claim
OpenAI may update these Terms at any time; for materially adverse changes, at least 30 days' notice will be given via email or in-product notification, after which users must stop using Services if they disagree; other changes take effect immediately upon posting.

## Why It Matters
Users must monitor Terms changes or risk losing the ability to opt out. Businesses building on OpenAI need processes to track policy updates and evaluate impact on their operations.
