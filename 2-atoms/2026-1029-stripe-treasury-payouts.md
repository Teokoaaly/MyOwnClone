## Claim
Stripe Treasury payouts cost **$1.50 per local bank payout** and **$1.50 per debit card payout**, plus **0.75% of the payout amount** (minimum 50¢ fee), with funding available through ACH or wire.

## Why It Matters
The 0.75% percentage fee on payout amounts (with 50¢ minimum) means that large payouts cost significantly more than the flat $1.50 fee, while small payouts are dominated by the minimum fee. ACH funding has no additional fees beyond the per-payout cost, while wire funding incurs a $2.00 per-wire fee, making ACH the cost-effective choice for moderate-to-large volumes.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] requires a card upfront for trial conversions, which creates onboarding friction for new businesses. Treasury payouts serve companies that have already graduated past the trial stage and need to move funds to recipients — the 0.75% fee on large payouts can create unpredictable cash-out costs compared to the flat $1.50 fee for small, frequent distributions.
