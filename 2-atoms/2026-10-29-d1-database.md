## Claim

D1 database stored data costs $0.50 per GB-month after the first 1 GB is free.

## Why It Matters

D1 brings SQLite-based serverless SQL to the Workers platform. The 1 GB free tier covers small databases, and at $0.50/GB-month it is more expensive than R2's $0.015/GB-month, but the tradeoff is immediate SQL query capability without managing a separate database service. Read/write request pricing further shapes the cost model for query-heavy workloads.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-29-r2-storage]] charges $0.015/GB-month after 10 GB-month free, while [[2026-10-29-d1-database]] charges $0.50/GB-month after 1 GB free. D1 is 33x more expensive per GB-month for stored data, but D1 includes query functionality (read/write requests at $0.50/M and $5.00/M respectively) that R2 lacks, making the cost comparison dependent on whether the workload needs SQL semantics or just raw storage.
