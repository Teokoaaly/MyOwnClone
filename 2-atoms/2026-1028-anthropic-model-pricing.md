## Claim
Anthropic model pricing per MTok ranges from the fastest/cheapest models to the most capable, with per-token rates that scale by intelligence level. The cheapest model has the lowest per-MTok rate but limited capability, while top-tier models command higher rates for complex reasoning tasks. Model selection is the primary cost driver in the Anthropic pricing structure.

## Why It Matters
Model pricing per MTok means that moving from a cheaper to a more capable model can increase costs 3-5x for the same workload. Teams must balance capability needs against budget constraints, selecting the cheapest model that meets performance requirements. This is especially relevant for multi-tenant SaaS platforms where per-tenant model allocation affects margin structure.
