## Claim
Before generating a temporary address, users can choose Domain, +Gmail, or Gmail dot formats so the address matches exactly what the signup form accepts.

## Why It Matters
Gmail ignores dots in the part before @ and everything after a +. So jo.hn@gmail.com and john+shop@gmail.com both reach the inbox for john@gmail.com. This allows creating many different addresses that look unique to a signup form while all delivering to the same real inbox.
