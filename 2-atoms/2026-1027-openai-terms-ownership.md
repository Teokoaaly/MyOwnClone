## Claim
As between the user and OpenAI, the user retains ownership rights in Input and owns the Output; OpenAI assigns its right, title, and interest in Output to the user, though output may not be unique and other users may receive similar output.

## Why It Matters
Clear ownership terms help users understand what rights they have over AI output, especially for commercial or creative use. The non-uniqueness caveat means users should not assume exclusive rights to generated content.
