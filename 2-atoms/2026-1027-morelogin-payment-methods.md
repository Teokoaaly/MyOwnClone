## Claim

MoreLogin accepts payments via PayPal, Card, Coinbase, Visa, Mastercard, and more — supporting multiple payment methods including cryptocurrency through Coinbase to accommodate diverse user preferences and regional payment infrastructure.

## Why It Matters

Wide payment method availability reduces friction for global users who may have limited access to traditional card networks; supporting PayPal, major credit cards, and cryptocurrency ensures users across different jurisdictions can subscribe to paid plans without payment gateway barriers.

## [FRICTION]

> No direct friction with other atoms, as payment method availability is orthogonal to profile limits, feature tiers, or cloud phone billing — users on Free or Pro plans can choose from the same payment suite, and no atom contradicts this claim. However, regional availability disparities (e.g., Coinbase access in restricted jurisdictions) could create implicit friction for individual users, though this is outside the vault's scope and marked for awareness only.
