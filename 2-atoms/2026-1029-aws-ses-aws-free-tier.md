## Claim
New AWS customers receive up to $200 in AWS Free Tier credits, which can be applied toward eligible services including Amazon SES; the free plan is available for 6 months after account creation, and any remaining credit balance automatically applies to paid plans if the customer upgrades.

## Why It Matters
The 6-month, $200 cap means early-stage startups can significantly offset SES costs during their first half-year, but must plan for full pricing after the free tier expires.
