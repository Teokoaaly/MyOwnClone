## Claim

Enterprise plan offers custom pricing for 10 TB+ data size and unlimited bandwidth, includes all Prod Pack features (SLA, SOC 2 Type 2, Prometheus/Grafana/Datadog insights, multi-zone HA, Insights), dedicated professional support, a dedicated technical account manager, unlimited databases, HIPAA compliance, VPC peering, SSO integration, and custom contract options.

## Why It Matters

Enterprise is tailored for large organizations with strict compliance, performance, and operational needs — the custom pricing and dedicated support justify the cost for regulated industries, but the entry barrier is high and pricing transparency is low.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1029-upstash-storage-bandwidth]] (escrita hoy) storage cost $0,25/GB beyond 1GB free — es incierto si este pricing se aplica a Enterprise o si hay exenciones a medida, lo que fricciona con la promesa de "10 TB+" sin referencia de costos claros.

> [[2026-1029-upstash-prod-pack]] (por crear) Prod Pack añade SLA, SOC 2, monitoring a cualquier plan menos Free — Enterprise incluye Prod Pack por defecto, pero la pregunta es si las features de Prod Pack añaden valor real encima de Enterprise custom, o si son simplemente features estandarizadas que Enterprise ya incluye.

Ambas no pueden ser completamente correctas: o los costos de storage se aplican transversalmente y Enterprise los presupuesta como línea item, o Enterprise negocia exenciones completas y la estructura $0,25/GB no le es aplicable. Marcado para juicio humano, no auto-resuelto.
