## Claim
Deepgram's pricing is per-minute of transcribed audio with volume-based discounts, creating a usage-based cost model where cost scales with audio duration rather than per-user seats.

## Why It Matters
Unlike the per-seat cost dominance model validated in `costes-por-categoria-tenant`, Deepgram's cost scales with transcribed minutes, not user count. For products with audio transcription needs, cost depends on average audio duration per user per month. If each user generates 2 hours of audio daily, monthly minutes per user = 60 × 2 × 30 = 3,600 minutes. At Deepgram's rates, this could exceed per-user SaaS costs quickly. The friction is in comparing event-based pricing (Deepgram) against per-seat models: the break-even point requires measuring audio duration per active user, and for high-volume audio products, Deepgram may be more expensive than a per-seat alternative with included transcription.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1027-proton-business-vpn-essentials-6-99-minimo-2-usuarios]] includes NetShield ad/malware blocking and always-on VPN at $6.99/user/mo with min 2 users, while Deepgram charges per transcribed minute with no user minimum. Both represent valid cost models for different use cases, but a product adding audio transcription must choose between per-seat pricing (where transcription cost is amortized across users) and per-minute pricing (where cost scales with actual usage). The decision hinges on the audio-per-user ratio, which requires empirical measurement before committing to a pricing model.

## Nota de extensión 2026-11-03
Las tres capturas de `deepgram.com/pricing` de esta corrida (2026-10-31, 2026-11-01, 2026-11-02) **confirman la afirmación de este átomo y la afianzan con cifras concretas**. Deepgram sí publica precios **por minuto**: Flux STT English y Nova-3 Monolingual a **$0,0077/min** pay-as-you-go ($0,0065 en Growth), Flux STT Multilingual a **$0,0078/min**, Nova-3 Multilingual a **$0,0092/min**; los add-ons van aparte por minuto (Redaction y Speaker Diarization $0,0020/min, Entity Detection $0,0017/min, Keyterm Prompting $0,0013/min, Smart Formatting incluido), hay **$200 de crédito gratuito** (la FAQ dice "Roughly 43,000 minutes, over 700 hours"), el plan Growth pide **$4.000+/año** con hasta un 20 % de descuento y sobreconsumo al **+10 %**, y el cobro es **por segundo sin redondeo**, con el multicanal facturado por la duración sumada entre canales. El detalle nuevo y relevante es que la página **mixea unidades en la misma página**: las tablas van en $/min y la FAQ en $/hr ($0,46/hr monolingual, $0,39/hr Growth, $0,55/hr multilingüe) — lo que permite la comparación honesta que este átomo pedía.
