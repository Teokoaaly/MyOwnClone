## Claim

The Gologin Business plan costs $49.50/month for 300 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

The Business tier at $49.50/mo for 300 profiles brings the price-per-profile down to $0.165, making it suitable for growing teams or agencies that need a moderate number of isolated profiles. This represents a significant cost-per-profile reduction compared to the Professional tier, enabling profile-based workflows at scale that would be impractical at lower tiers.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-professional-25-9]] notes 25 profiles for $9/mo. The price-per-profile at the Business tier ($0.165) is substantially lower than the Professional tier's $0.36 per profile, but the upfront commitment of 300 profiles may be excessive for individual users or small operations. Both can be correct for different user scales — the friction arises when a user's needs fall between these scales and cannot be cleanly assigned to one tier without over- or under-provisioning.
