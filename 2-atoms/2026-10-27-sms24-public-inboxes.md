## Claim
Messages in SMS24.me public inboxes are visible to everyone; no expectation of privacy or exclusivity surrounds any received SMS.

## Why It Matters
Users browsing available numbers must understand that any message received is public by design, making this service suitable only for non-sensitive verification flows where message exposure does not pose a risk.
