## Claim

Incogniton pricing has 4 tiers: **Starter Plus** $13.99/mo ($19.99 one-time) for 10 profiles with 1 free Cloud Phone (1 month), regular support and built-in proxies; **Entrepreneur** $20.99/mo ($29.99) for 50 profiles with Selenium/Puppeteer integration, API Access, Cookie Collector, Synchronizer and built-in proxies; **Professional** $55.99/mo ($79.99) for 150 profiles with 3 team member seats and all premium features; **Custom Package** $104.99+/mo ($149.99) for 500+ profiles with team seats, dedicated account manager and SLA. All plans offer 6-month (30% off) and yearly (40% off) discounts.

## Why It Matters

The pricing page uses a tiered model where lower tiers exclude advanced features (Cookie Collector, API, team management) that appear only in Entrepreneur and Professional tiers. This creates a feature-gated ecosystem: teams starting with Starter Plus must upgrade to access automation APIs or profile synchronization, while the Custom Package serves large operations needing profile/seed scaling. The 2-month free trial for Starter Plus that downgrades to 3 profiles after the period is a key conversion hook.
