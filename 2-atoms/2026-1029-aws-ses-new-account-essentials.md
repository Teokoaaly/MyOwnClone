## Claim
New SES accounts and account-region combinations with no metered SES activity since June 1, 2025 will start on the Essentials plan beginning July 21, 2026, but can upgrade or switch to à la carte pricing at any time.

## Why It Matters
New customers are defaulted to Essentials and must actively choose Pro or Enterprise, meaning most starters begin at the lowest cost tier before potentially upgrading for deliverability features or higher volume rates.
