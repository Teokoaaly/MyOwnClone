## Claim

Getsmscode positions itself as a service for receiving SMS online where users pay per SMS, explicitly stating "You can receive sms online and pay by per SMS" as its core proposition for privacy protection during internet registration.

## Why It Matters

The per-SMS pricing model combined with no-registration access lowers the barrier for one-time account verification while protecting user privacy. This model is distinct from free email services (like Mail.tm) that store messages indefinitely, and from subscription-based SMS services that require upfront commitment. The explicit pay-per-use stance makes the cost structure predictable for occasional users and distinguishes getsmscode in the temporary contact landscape.
