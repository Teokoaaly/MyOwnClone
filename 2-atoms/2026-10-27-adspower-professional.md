## Claim
The Professional plan offers 4 profile tiers: 10, 20, 50, and 100 profiles, with monthly or annual billing options.

## Why It Matters
The tiered profile structure allows users to scale from individual use (10 profiles) to medium-sized operations (100 profiles). The 4-profile increments provide granular control over resource allocation, and the annual billing option (40% discount) makes it cost-effective for committed users who need more than the Free plan's 2 profiles but don't yet require the Business tier.
