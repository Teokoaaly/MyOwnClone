## Claim
Langfuse uses graduated usage pricing where additional billable units beyond the included quota are charged at decreasing rates: $8/100k for 0-1M units, $7/100k for 1M-10M units, $6.50/100k for 10M-50M units, and $6/100k for 50M+ units, automatically providing lower effective rates as volume increases.

## Why It Matters
The graduated pricing model creates a natural economy of scale — larger projects pay significantly less per 100k units than smaller ones, incentivizing higher usage while keeping costs predictable. The tiered rate structure means a project using 5M units/month on Pro pays $551 total ($199 base + $72 next 900k at $8 + $280 next 4M at $7), versus $37 for 200k units on Core, demonstrating how volume dramatically affects the per-unit cost.

## [FRICTION]
Graduated pricing offers lower rates at higher volumes ($6-8/100k), yet the Enterprise tier at $2,499/month with yearly commitment can still reach $8,801/month at 100M units, where custom volume pricing becomes available. The fixed-tier plans cap at published graduated rates, and only Enterprise with committed contracts access the custom lowest tier ($6/100k for 50M+), creating a friction point where high-volume projects on fixed plans overpay compared to Enterprise customers with negotiated contracts.
