## Claim
OpenAI prohibits using services to cause, enable, facilitate, or encourage sexual abuse or exploitation of anyone under 18; child sexual abuse material (whether AI-generated or not); grooming, sextortion, or any form of conduct-based child sexual abuse; sexualizing children; exposing minors to age-inappropriate content (graphic self-harm, sexual, or violent content); promoting unhealthy dieting or exercise to minors; shaming or stigmatizing minors' body type or appearance; promoting or facilitating dangerous challenges or age-restricted goods/services to minors; and underaged sexual or violent roleplay.

## Why It Matters
Children and teens require special protection from AI-enabled harm. These prohibitions prevent the creation, distribution, or facilitation of child sexual abuse material, grooming, and other forms of sexual exploitation. They also guard minors from exposure to harmful content, unhealthy body-image practices, and dangerous viral challenges.
