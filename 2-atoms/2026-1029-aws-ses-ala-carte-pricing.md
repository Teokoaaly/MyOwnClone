## Claim
À la carte pricing allows customers to pay only for what they use with no minimum fees or mandatory service usage: outbound email $0.10/1K, global endpoints $0.03/1K, email validation $0.01/validation, tenants $0.005/tenant/month + $0.005/1K emails, dedicated IPs $24.95/IP/month, Mail Manager email processing $0.15/1K, and archiving $2/GB ingested + $0.19/GB/month storage and search.

## Why It Matters
À la carte is optimal for unpredictable workloads or customers who need only specific features (e.g., inbound processing without outbound sending), since there are no plan-level minimums or fixed fees beyond per-resource charges.
