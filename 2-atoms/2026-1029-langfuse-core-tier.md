## Claim
The Langfuse Core tier at $29/month is designed for production projects, offering 100k units/month included, unlimited users, 90-day data access, in-app support, and graduated additional usage pricing of $8/100k units (lower with volume).

## Why It Matters
Core represents the smallest paid entry point for production use cases, balancing cost with capabilities like unlimited users and extended data retention. The $8/100k graduated pricing mechanism means costs scale predictably with usage, making it suitable for growing projects that exceed Hobby limits but don't yet need Pro's advanced features or SLA guarantees.

## [FRICTION]
Core includes unlimited users and 90-day data access at $29/month, while Hobby caps at 2 users and 30 days for free. The jump from 2 to unlimited users and 30 to 90 days data access represents a meaningful value increase that may not justify the monthly cost for solo developers or very small projects, creating a pricing discontinuity at the Hobby-Core boundary.
