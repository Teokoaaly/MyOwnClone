## Claim
OpenAI prohibits using services for threats, intimidation, harassment, or defamation; suicide, self-harm, or disordered eating promotion; sexual violence or non-consensual intimate content; terrorism or violence including hate-based violence; weapons development, procurement, or use; illicit activities; system compromise or property breach including IP infringement; real money gambling; and building AI tools that provide tailored advice requiring a license without licensed professional involvement.

## Why It Matters
These prohibitions establish the baseline acceptable-use boundary for all OpenAI users. They protect individuals and society from harm while defining the legal and ethical perimeter within which AI tools may operate. Violations risk loss of access and other penalties.
