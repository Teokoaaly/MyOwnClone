## Claim

SMS-MAN offers free virtual numbers through its Telegram channel, but all toll-free numbers are public and anyone can read the text messages sent to them.

## Why It Matters

Users can test the service without a deposit using free numbers from the Telegram channel, but must be aware that these numbers have no privacy — any message received is visible to anyone monitoring the channel. This makes free numbers suitable only for non-sensitive verifications or testing, while paid numbers should be used for any account where message confidentiality is required. The Telegram bot alternative (t.me/Smsbyliknot_bot) may offer increased privacy by allowing multiple phone number requests in one click, though the public nature of toll-free numbers remains a limitation.
