## Claim
The Scale plan has a typical monthly spend of $701 based on high load with 100 GB of storage, offering $0.222 per CU-hr, $0.35 per GB-month, and compute sizes up to 56 CU (224 GB RAM) with SLAs, HIPAA, and private network support.

## Why It Matters
This plan is designed for production applications with sustained traffic, higher data volumes, and more complex queries requiring consistent compute resources, including guaranteed availability and compliance certifications.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-29-neon-pricing-launchplan]] typical spend of $15/mo is based on intermittent load with 1 GB, while [[2026-10-29-neon-pricing-scaleplan]] typical spend of $701/mo is based on high load with 100 GB. The jump from 1 GB to 100 GB in storage costs alone at $0.35/GB-month adds $35/mo, yet the total spend difference is $686/mo — suggesting the compute cost difference (700+ CU-hrs vs. ~15 CU-hrs) is the primary driver, not storage.
