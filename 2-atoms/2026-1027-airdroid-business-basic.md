## Claim
AirDroid Business Basic costs $12 per device/year and provides a Basic Remote Access Suite with Basic Management Features.

## Why It Matters
At $12/device/year, AirDroid Business Basic offers the most affordable entry point for enterprise device management, undercutting competitors like TeamViewer and AnyDesk which charge premium rates for similar remote access capabilities. This pricing makes basic MDM features accessible to small businesses with limited IT budgets.

## [FRICTION]
> The AirDroid Business pricing page lists Basic at $12/device/year, but does not disclose whether this is a flat fee or scales with device count beyond the "per device" designation, creating ambiguity for budget planning at scale.
