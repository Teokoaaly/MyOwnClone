## Claim

Normal (simple) captcha costs $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 solves with a 1-second solving speed and 12,000 free capacity per minute.

## Why It Matters

Normal captcha offers the highest free capacity per minute (12,000) and tied-for-fastest solving speed (1 second), making it the most cost-effective choice for high-volume solvers. The price range of $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 is competitive, undercutting most modern captcha types while maintaining the fastest throughput. This makes it the default choice when volume and free capacity are prioritized over captcha type specificity.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-26-image-captcha]] asserts Image Captcha costs $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 with 1-second solving speed and 12,000 free capacity per minute. Both share identical price, speed, and capacity metrics. Both cannot be distinguished by these metrics alone; the distinction may rest on captcha subtype or detection heuristics not captured in the price table.
