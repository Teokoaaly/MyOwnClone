## Claim

Resend includes 3 inboxes on all plans; additional inboxes cost $1/mo each on paid plans, with inbox emails counting against the transactional quota, and pay-as-you-go must be enabled to add inboxes beyond the free tier.

## Why It Matters

The inbox model is a recurring cost that scales with team size. While all plans include 3 inboxes, the $1/inbox/mo charge on paid plans means a 5-person team needs 5 inboxes = $2/mo extra on top of the plan base. On the Free plan, the 3 inboxes are included but pay-as-you-go is not available — if more inboxes are needed, the user must upgrade to a paid plan. For growing teams, the inbox cost is modest but additive: a 10-person team with 10 inboxes pays $7/mo total ($6 for additional inboxes + $1 minimum plan consideration), which over a year equals $84 — enough to upgrade from Free to Pro or shift the Pro vs Scale calculus. Teams should count inboxes as part of total cost of ownership, not as a freebie.

## Nota de extensión 2026-11-03
Verificado contra `sources/2026-10-29-resend-pricing.md`: el claim es correcto —3 inboxes incluidos en todos los planes, $1/inbox/mes adicionales en Pro y Scale, los emails de inbox consumen la cuota transaccional y el pay-as-you-go es requisito para añadir inboxes.

Dato adicional de la misma captura: **cada email recibido también cuenta** contra la cuota transaccional ("Each received email counts towards your transactional emails quota"). Es decir, un inbox de soporte que recibe 500 consultas/mes consume 500 de los 3.000 emails del plan Free. En Enterprise, el número de inboxes es "Flexible", lo que rompe el modelo de coste incremental por buzón y abre la puerta a un contrato distinto — ver [[2026-1029-resend-plan-enterprise]].
