## Claim
The Multilogin Free plan has no time limit and requires no credit card. It includes 5 cloud mobile and browser profiles, a one-time bonus of 200 MB premium proxy traffic, and 1 cloud mobile profile with 30 minutes of use. Forever free.

## Why It Matters
The free plan serves as the entry point for users to explore the platform before upgrading. It anchors the bottom of the pricing tier and distinguishes Multilogin from competitors that require credit card upfront. The 30-minute one-time mobile minutes and 200 MB one-time proxy traffic give immediate value without commitment.

## [FRICTION]
> Users often confuse the Free plan's one-time bonuses (200 MB proxy, 30 min mobile) with monthly allowances included in paid plans. The free plan has no recurring monthly proxy or mobile minutes — these are one-time only. Paid plans start at $7.08/mo annually and include monthly proxy traffic and mobile minutes allowances that roll over each month.

> The free plan includes 5 profile limits, while paid Pro plans start at 10 profiles and Business plans start at 100 profiles. Users upgrading from Free may underestimate the profile limit jump needed for their workflow.
