## Claim
The feature comparison matrix shows outbound sending (✓ on all plans), virtual deliverability manager (✓ on all, global add-on), dedicated IPs (Pro includes 1 managed, Enterprise includes 5+), email validation (add-on on Pro, included on Enterprise), global endpoints (add-on on Pro/Pro, included on Enterprise), and tenants (add-on on Pro/Enterprise, 1K included + $0.005/tenant/month extra).

## Why It Matters
The feature matrix drives plan selection beyond pure cost-per-thousand: teams needing global deliverability, dedicated IPs, or tenant isolation will choose Pro or Enterprise even if Essentials has a lower per-1K rate.
