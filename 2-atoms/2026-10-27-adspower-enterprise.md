## Claim
The Enterprise plan supports 5000+ profiles with customizable configuration and price upon request, designed for large organizations with specific needs.

## Why It Matters
The Enterprise tier is the only AdsPower plan that isn't publicly priced, requiring direct contact for quotes. This tier accommodates very large-scale operations (5000+ profiles) that would be impractical with the tiered Professional or Business plans. The "customizable on demand" approach means features, profile limits, and pricing are negotiated per organization, making it suitable for established businesses with specific compliance, security, or operational requirements that standard plans can't meet.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-22-priceai-official-prices]] mentions enterprise custom pricing for PriceAI but doesn't specify profile counts or per-profile costs. Both notes describe enterprise-level pricing that requires direct contact, but AdsPower Enterprise focuses on profile count (5000+) while PriceAI enterprise targets API usage volume — different metrics and use cases mean both can correctly describe their respective models without contradiction.
