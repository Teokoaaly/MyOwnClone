## Claim

## Why It Matters

Users can register on online services without exposing their personal phone number. The extensive service and country coverage, combined with flexible rental periods (one-time up to 20 minutes, rental up to 90 days), makes SMS-MAN accessible globally for account verification, particularly useful for job searching, online dating, selling items, and managing rentals where privacy is desired. The API enables professionals to automate bulk verification workflows.
