## Claim
Annual billing provides a 40% discount on AdsPower's Professional and Business plans, reducing the effective monthly rate from $9 to $5.40/mo (Professional) and from $61 to $36.60/mo (Business).

## Why It Matters
The 40% yearly discount makes long-term commitment financially attractive for serious users. At the Professional level, annual payment drops the cost from $108/year ($9 × 12) to $64.8/year, a savings of $43.20. At the Business level, it drops from $732/year ($61 × 12) to $439.2/year, a savings of $292.80. This pricing structure rewards stability and predictable usage patterns, encouraging users to commit to longer-term plans rather than month-to-month billing.
