## Claim
SMS24.me lists 53 countries with 9,865 public inboxes, providing the broadest country selection among free SMS receive services.

## Why It Matters
Users needing verification codes from region-specific services can select numbers from supported countries, enabling registration on platforms that restrict SMS to certain geographies. This wide coverage makes SMS24.me useful for international users who may face regional restrictions with other free SMS services.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-27-free-sms-receive-countries]] asserts that Free-SMS-Receive provides temporary phone numbers across multiple countries including Netherlands, France, Germany, Spain, Austria, United Kingdom, Hong Kong, and China, supporting international SMS verification. Free-SMS-Receive lists 8 core countries while SMS24.me lists 53 countries with 9,865 inboxes, representing a an order-of-magnitude broader coverage. Both can be correct — they serve different user needs (curated small set versus extensive catalog), but the coverage gap may influence a user's choice when a required country is only available on one service.
