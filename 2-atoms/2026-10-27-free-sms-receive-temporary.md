## Claim
Free-SMS-Receive provides disposable phone numbers that are changed every month, with new temporary numbers regularly added to the platform.

## Why It Matters
The rotating number scheme means users cannot rely on the same number for ongoing communication, but it is well-suited for one-time verification codes. This model prevents number hoarding and keeps the service fresh, though users must check for new numbers if they need repeated verification.
