## Claim
Mail.tm Temp Mail API explicitly prohibits reselling of its temp mail service.

## Why It Matters
The no-reselling policy ensures the service remains a personal temp mail solution rather than becoming a commercialized resale platform, preserving its intended use case for individual developers and users who need disposable email addresses.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-26-mail-tm-limite-de-8-qps-por-ip-address]] asserts an 8 QPS rate limit per IP. Both statements are compatible — the resale prohibition governs distribution rights while the QPS limit governs per-IP request volume.
