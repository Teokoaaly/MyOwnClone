## Claim
AirDroid Business Standard costs $21 per device/year and provides a Complete Remote Access Suite & Management features.

## Why It Matters
At $21/device/year, AirDroid Business Standard positions itself in the mid-tier of remote device management pricing, offering a balance between cost and functionality for growing businesses that need more than basic remote access but do not require enterprise-grade MDM features.

## [FRICTION]
> The $21/device/year price point for Standard sits between Basic ($12) and Enterprise ($33), yet the feature differentiation between Standard and Enterprise is not explicitly quantified on the pricing page, making it difficult for customers to justify the 57% price jump for potentially limited additional features.
