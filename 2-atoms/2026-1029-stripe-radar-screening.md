## Claim
Stripe Radar charges **starting at $0.05 per screened transaction**, with tiered pricing available for higher-volume platforms seeking predictable monthly pricing.

## Why It Matters
Radar's per-transaction screening fee provides AI-powered fraud protection across all payment methods, with costs scaling directly with transaction volume. At $0.05 per transaction, a business processing 10,000 monthly transactions pays $500/month — comparable to fixed monthly plans but more predictable for variable volume, while low-volume businesses benefit from pay-as-you-go pricing without a minimum monthly commitment.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] requires a card upfront for trial conversions, which creates onboarding friction. Radar screening can be activated after trial completion, but the $0.05 per-transaction cost adds to the 2.9% + 30¢ base payment fee, meaning trial businesses must weigh fraud protection costs against the risk of chargebacks during the early, unprotected period.
