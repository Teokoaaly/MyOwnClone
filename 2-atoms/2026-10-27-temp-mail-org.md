## Claim

Temp-mail.org provides a completely free, no-registration disposable email service that instantly generates temporary addresses for receiving verification emails, with no daily quotas or hidden charges.

## Why It Matters

Users who need to avoid spam while registering for online services can use temp-mail.org to keep their real inbox clean. The instant generation and unlimited use makes it ideal for one-time verifications, testing, and protecting privacy without creating another permanent email account.
