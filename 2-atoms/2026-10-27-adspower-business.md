## Claim
The Business plan is the "Popular" tier priced at $61.0/mo (or $36.6/mo with annual billing) and $439.2/year, designed for teams and power users needing more than the Professional tier offers.

## Why It Matters
The Business tier bridges the gap between the Professional plan's 100-profile limit and the Enterprise plan's custom pricing. At $61/mo for what's labeled "Popular," it represents the high-usage individual or small team sweet spot. The 40% annual discount ($36.6/mo effective rate) makes it viable for ongoing operations, and the inclusion of "unlimited profile sharing" and "specific permissions to members" enables team collaboration that the Professional plan's more basic team features don't support at scale.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-22-priceai-official-prices]] states that PriceAI's official API pricing starts at $19/mo for 1000 tokens, with enterprise custom pricing available. Both notes discuss "Popular" or high-usage tiers for browser/automation tools, but AdsPower Business at $61/mo targets antidetect browser/team management use cases while PriceAI targets AI API usage — different problem spaces make both potentially correct for their respective domains.
