## Claim

Receive-smss does not retain SMS messages longer than 24 hours.

## Why It Matters

Users who are concerned about message persistence can use Receive-smss knowing that verification codes are automatically removed after 24 hours, reducing the risk of old codes being accessed or used maliciously. This ephemeral approach aligns with privacy-conscious use cases where messages should not remain accessible indefinitely.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-free-sms-receive-privacy]] asserts that Free-SMS-Receive also deletes messages after 24 hours, whereas Receive-smss makes the same 24-hour retention claim. Both services commit to 24-hour automatic deletion, reinforcing that this timeframe is a shared design choice for temporary SMS verification. However, the identical retention policy means this concern does not differentiate between the two services for users prioritizing message ephemerality. Marcado para juicio humano, no auto-resuelto.
