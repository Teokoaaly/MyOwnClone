## Claim
Stripe enables customers to pay with stablecoins at **1.5% of the transaction amount in USD**, including conversion to fiat, wallet and AML screening, fraud prevention, and gas sponsorship.

## Why It Matters
Stablecoin pricing introduces a 1.5% fee that bundles currency conversion, compliance, and fraud prevention into a single rate — distinct from the 0.8% ACH Direct Debit rate and the 2.9% + 30¢ card rates, offering businesses a crypto-native alternative with built-in risk tools.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] describes a 30-day trial with card requirement as the self-service onboarding path, while stablecoin payments bypass traditional card rails entirely. These are complementary paths — one fiat-on-ramp with card friction, the other crypto-on-ramp with different fee structure — and both can coexist in a multi-rail strategy.
