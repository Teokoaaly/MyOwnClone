## Claim
Proxy-Cheap pricing limitations include: per-plan limits requiring Support contact for increases, volume pricing available at 4 GB and 51 GB thresholds for Rotating Residential, minimum 3 GB order gate, no VAT added to prices but payment fees apply to most methods (card, PayPal, crypto, iDEAL, Przelewy24, Kakao Pay, Naver Pay, PAYCO, Samsung Pay), and balance top-ups from balance have no payment fee.

## Why It Matters
The volume pricing thresholds create step-cost structures that may not align with steady consumption, and the 3 GB minimum excludes light users. Payment fee transparency at checkout vs. balance-free top-ups creates a two-tier cost structure users must navigate. The no-VAT policy is advantageous for international buyers, but the proliferating payment method list with varying fees introduces cost uncertainty.

## [FRICTION]
[[2026-1027-proxy-cheap-use-cases]] Different use cases demand different proxy types and volume patterns: data scraping startups may hit the 3 GB minimum and need 4 GB/51 GB volume thresholds, while occasional sneaker coppers may find the minimums excessive; the payment fee variation across methods (card vs. balance vs. crypto) adds a hidden cost layer that influences provider choice beyond just proxy pricing.
