## Claim
cto.new's Agency in a Box feature enables a one-person agency to sell logos, pitch decks, or website audits, with AI agents doing the actual work, effectively productizing service businesses through AI.

## Why It Matters
Productizing agency services through AI agents lowers the marginal cost of delivering high-value creative work, allowing founders to scale service revenue without hiring a team or building custom tooling.
