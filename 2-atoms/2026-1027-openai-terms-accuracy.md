## Claim
Artificial intelligence output may not always be accurate; users must evaluate Output for accuracy and appropriateness before use, and must not rely on Output as a sole source of truth or substitute for professional advice.

## Why It Matters
Given the probabilistic nature of machine learning, output can be incorrect, incomplete, or offensive. Users in high-stakes contexts (credit, education, employment, housing, insurance, legal, medical) must incorporate human review.
