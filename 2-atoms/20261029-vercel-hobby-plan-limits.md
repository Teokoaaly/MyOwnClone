## Claim
The Hobby plan at $0/mo includes strict usage caps and does not allow purchasing additional usage beyond the included credits — Pro plans receive $20/mo included credit with pay-as-you-go uncapped add-ons, while Hobby users are limited to the usage caps and cannot upgrade pay-as-you-go.

## Why It Matters
This is the primaryplan boundary: Hobby is for personal, non-commercial use only. The inability to purchase additional usage means projects that outgrow the caps must migrate to Pro or Enterprise, creating a hard ceiling for growing side projects and small businesses that start on Hobby.

## [FRICTION]
> [[20261029-vercel-pro-plan-features]] (escrita hace ~21 semanas) afirma que el plan Pro incluye "$20 of included usage credit" con "Paid add-on" resources available uncapped. Este átomo presiona esa creencia porque la documentación indica explícitamente que las cuentas Hobby están "limitadas a los caps de uso y no pueden comprar uso adicional"; las dos afirmaciones no pueden ser simultáneamente ciertas: o Hobby puede comprar uso adicional (contradiciendo la FAQ Q6), o no puede (que es lo que dice la FAQ).

> [[2026-10-29-vercel-pricing.md]] FAQ Q6: "If you have a Hobby account, you are limited to the usage caps and cannot purchase additional usage." Este átomo confirma la FAQ sobre Hobby caps, pero entra en fricción con la afirmación del átomo Pro sobre crédito ilimitado.
