## Claim

The Free plan is available forever at $0/month and includes 2 profiles and 2 users with no credit card required, providing a safe and antidetect browser profile, Chrome & Firefox support kept up-to-date, real canvas fingerprint, online technical support, simple easy-to-use UX, powerful team collaboration, profile transfer, safe and stable proxy available for purchase, powerful and easy-to-use synchroniser, Local API, and browser automation with Selenium or Puppeteer.

## Why It Matters

The free forever plan serves as the entry point for users to experience MoreLogin's core capabilities without financial commitment, lowering the barrier to adoption and allowing individuals or small-scale operations to test the platform before deciding to upgrade to paid tiers.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-pro-plan]] states the Pro plan is $5.4/month (40% off $9/month) with 10 profiles and 10 users. The Free plan limits to 2 profiles and 2 users, creating a significant feature gap that may pressure users to upgrade sooner than needed for growing operations.

> This note presiona esa creencia porque el plan Free incluye "Profile transfer" y "Powerful Team Collaboration" en su lista de características incluidas, sugeriendo que el trabajo en equipo no está exclusivamente detrás de un pago, pero la limitación de perfiles (2 vs 10) contradicts the claim that team collaboration is "powerful" at the Free tier — o el comodín cubre en la práctica solo 2 perfiles, o la nota de colaboración sobreestima la utilidad práctica del Free plan para equipos.
