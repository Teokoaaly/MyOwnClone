## Claim

Getsmscode offers four user tier levels — Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Ultimate — with increasing discounts of 10%, 15%, 20%, and 35% respectively, requiring minimum topups of $5, $20, $100, and $500 to qualify.

## Why It Matters

The tiered discount structure rewards higher spending, reducing the effective per-SMS cost for users who need many verification codes. This model incentivizes bulk topup for frequent users while maintaining pay-per-SMS accessibility for occasional users. The 35% Ultimate tier discount is the deepest in the SMS-receiving category, making it attractive for phone farm operators running thousands of verifications.
