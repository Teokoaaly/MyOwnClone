## Claim
Multilogin offers a referral program where users on paid plans can earn 15% from eligible plan payments and renewals made by referred users. Referral rewards become available after a 45-day waiting period and can be used toward Multilogin plans, mobile minutes, or proxy traffic. Referral rewards cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another account. Earnings continue from successful renewals for as long as the referred user's subscription remains active.

## Why It Matters
The referral program provides a recurring income stream for influential users or agencies. The 15% recurring commission on renewals creates long-term passive income. The 45-day waiting period and non-withdrawable rewards mean referrals are a medium-to-long-term commitment, not quick cash. The ability to redeem rewards for plans/minutes/proxy makes it most valuable for active Multilogin users.

## [FRICTION]
> The 45-day waiting period before rewards become available creates a lag between referral signup and earning. Users referring many users may find the cash flow gap significant if they expect immediate returns.

> Referral rewards cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred — they must be used toward Multilogin services. Users who wanted cash liquidity or to transfer rewards to other accounts find this restriction limiting. The rewards are most valuable for existing Multilogin users who can apply them to their own plans or minutes.

> The 15% commission applies to "eligible plan payments and renewals." It is unclear whether this includes add-on purchases (proxy traffic, mobile minutes, pay-as-you-go) or only base plan payments. Users maximizing referral earnings may need to clarify what counts as "eligible."

> Referral rewards expire or become unavailable if the referred user cancels their subscription. Users relying on referral credits for their own plan payments may lose those rewards if referred users churn quickly.
