## Claim

The Gologin Custom plan starts at $139.50/month for 2000 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

At $139.50/mo for 2000 profiles, the price-per-profile is $0.06975, which undercuts the Enterprise tier's $0.0895 per profile at 1000. This makes the Custom plan the most cost-effective option for users needing 2000+ profiles, as the per-profile cost continues the downward pricing trend at scale. Organizations requiring large numbers of profiles can achieve significant savings compared to the Enterprise tier.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-enterprise-179]] notes $179/mo for the Enterprise higher tier. The Custom plan at $139.50/mo for 2000 profiles costs less in total than the $179 Enterprise tier, yet the source does not specify the profile count for the $179 tier. If the $179 Enterprise tier provides more than approximately 1780 profiles, the Enterprise tier offers a lower price-per-profile; if it provides fewer, the Custom plan is more cost-effective. The user must determine the profile count attached to the $179 tier to make an informed choice.
