## Claim

R2 Standard Storage costs $0.015 per GB-month after the first 10 GB-month are free.

## Why It Matters

R2's egress-free model eliminates the bandwidth costs that make S3 expensive for many workloads. The 10 GB-month free tier covers small projects, and at $0.015/GB-month, stored data remains very affordable even at medium scales. The class A/B operation pricing further distinguishes between list-heavy and read-heavy access patterns.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-29-d1-database]] stores data at $0.50/GB-month after 1 GB free, while [[2026-10-29-r2-storage]] charges only $0.015/GB-month after 10 GB-month free. R2 is 33x cheaper for stored data, but D1 includes a built-in SQL database layer that R2 does not, creating a tradeoff between pure storage cost and database functionality.
