## Claim

Cloudflare Turnstile captcha costs $1.45 per 1,000 solves with a 2-second solving speed and 2,883 free capacity per minute.

## Why It Matters

Turnstile is positioned as a mid-price option among modern captcha types. Its solving speed of 2 seconds per 1,000 makes it faster than many legacy captcha types but more expensive per solve than basic image captchas. The 2,883 free capacity per minute is relatively low compared to simpler captcha types, suggesting it may be more resource-intensive to solve or less prioritized in the free tier.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-26-image-captcha]] asserts Image Captcha costs $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 with 1-second solving speed and 12,000 free capacity per minute. Turnstile's solving speed is double (2 sec) and its free capacity is roughly 4x lower (2,883 vs 12,000). Both cannot be the fastest and highest-caption free type simultaneously.
