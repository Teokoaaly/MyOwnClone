## Claim
OpenAI's aggregate liability under these Terms will not exceed the greater of the amount paid for the service during the 12 months before the liability arose or $100; disclaimers exclude indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or exemplary damages including loss of profits, goodwill, use, or data.

## Why It Matters
Users exposed to risk through OpenAI Services have limited recourse for major losses. This cap affects businesses building on the platform who should maintain their own insurance and risk management.
