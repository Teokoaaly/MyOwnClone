## Claim
Pricing is usually cheaper than competitors and aims to match the cheapest at every scale. PostHog's free tier doesn't expire, includes full access to most features, resets every month, no credit card or sales call required. Beyond the all-in-one advantage, most competitors offer time-limited free trials (14-30 days) with restricted features.

## Why It Matters
The combination of a truly free forever tier + no minimum spend + annual discounts makes PostHog the lowest-friction option for startups and established teams alike. Competitors' time-limited trials create urgency but also risk of losing data or workflow when the trial ends, whereas PostHog's model is sustainable long-term.
