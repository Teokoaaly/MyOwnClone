## Claim
Pay-as-you-go pricing: same free tier every month, unlimited usage, 6 projects (up from 1), 7-year data retention (up from 1 year), email support or Slack over $2k/mo. Most products get their own billing limit — going viral won't require another funding round.

## Why It Matters
The pay-as-you-go tier bridges the gap between the generous free limits and enterprise needs, offering more projects and longer retention without requiring a contract. Users only pay for what they use above the free allowance, making costs predictable and scalable.
