## Claim
Multilogin Cloud Phones are billed based on active usage time. The timer runs only while the cloud phone is active (launched). Each plan includes a monthly mobile minutes allowance. Once included minutes are used up, additional minutes can be purchased as pay-as-you-go add-ons starting at $0.0073 per minute. The free plan includes 30 mobile minutes as a one-time bonus; paid plans include a monthly allowance based on the selected plan. A single session can run as long as needed, but Multilogin only bills the first 180 minutes of any uninterrupted session — runtime after the first 180 minutes is free. Auto-stop can be configured to pause sessions after a set time.

## Why It Matters
Cloud phone billing is usage-based and granular. The 180-minute per-session billing cap is a key cost-control feature — users with long-running sessions effectively get those minutes free. Auto-stop adds another cost-control layer. The difference between one-time bonus minutes (Free) and monthly allowances (Paid) shapes the upgrade path.

## [FRICTION]
> The "first 180 minutes billed, rest free" rule is counterintuitive. Users may assume all session time is billed, or conversely may not realize that extending a session beyond 180 minutes stops billing entirely. This creates a behavioral incentive to either manually stop sessions at ~180 min or enable auto-stop, but users unaware of this rule may be surprised by billing patterns.

> Free plan's 30 minutes are one-time only — they do not renew monthly. Users who rely on Free plan cloud phones and use those 30 minutes may need to upgrade if they need continued mobile phone access, but the upgrade cost/value calculation is unclear without understanding the monthly allowances in each Pro/Business tier.

> Auto-stop configuration is profile-level but not immediately obvious in the dashboard. Users who forget to configure auto-stop and leave sessions running may consume minutes faster than expected, especially if they operate under the misconception that all session time is billed at the normal rate.
