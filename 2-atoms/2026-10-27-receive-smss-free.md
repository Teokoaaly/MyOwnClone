## Claim

Receive-smss is completely free with no daily limit on the number of SMS messages a user can receive.

## Why It Matters

Unlike some SMS receiving services that impose daily quotas or charge per message, Receive-smss allows users to receive an unlimited number of verification codes per day at no cost. This makes it particularly suitable for users who need to verify multiple accounts or repeatedly test registration flows without worrying about hitting a limit or incurring charges.
