## Claim
Stripe charges **$15.00 per dispute received** and **$15.00 per dispute countered manually**, with a **30% Smart Disputes fee** of the disputed amount for each dispute won (the $15 received fee still applies).

## Why It Matters
Dispute fees create a direct cost per chargeback dispute that can accumulate quickly for high-volume businesses. The 30% Smart Disputes fee on won disputes provides AI-assisted defense but adds a percentage cost on top of the fixed $15 received fee, making the total cost of disputes a combination of fixed and variable components.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] describes trial conversions that generate initial revenue, but dispute frequency depends on product quality and customer satisfaction — not on the pricing plan itself. High-ticket items may see fewer disputes than low-ticket impulse purchases, meaning the $15 per dispute fee disproportionately affects businesses with low average order values.
