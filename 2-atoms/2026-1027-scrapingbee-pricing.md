## Claim

ScrapingBee offers 5 tiered pricing plans: Hobby ($19/mo), Freelance ($49/mo), Startup ($99/mo), Business ($249/mo), and Business+ ($599/mo). All plans include API credits, concurrent requests, JavaScript rendering, rotating & premium proxies, geotargeting, screenshots & extraction rules, and dedicated scraping APIs. Higher tiers add priority email support, dedicated account managers, and team management. A free tier gives 1,000 API credits with no credit card required.

## Why It Matters

The clear tiered structure allows users to scale from personal projects (Hobby) to enterprise operations (Business+). The 1,000 free credits lower the entry barrier, while the progression from 10K to 120M API credits across plans reflects ScrapingBee's focus on headless browser + proxy infrastructure. The inclusion of JavaScript rendering and geotargeting across all paid tiers makes it competitive against API-only providers like Apify or RawProxy. Friction arises from the steep price jump between Startup ($99) and Business ($249), and the Enterprise+ features (dedicated account manager) requiring direct sales contact rather than self-serve signup.

## [FRICTION]

> The Hobby plan at $19/mo includes 10,000 API credits with JavaScript rendering, while the Freelance plan at $49/mo offers 30,000 credits — a 3x increase for 2.5x the price, suggesting the credit allocation is the primary differentiator rather than capability gaps (both include JS rendering, proxies, geotargeting). This pricing ratio pressures the belief that Hobby is sufficient for small-scale scraping, because 10K credits may be exhausted quickly when JavaScript rendering is active, pushing users toward Freelance prematurely. Both plans include the same feature set, so the price-per-credit efficiency favors Freelance at scale, but the mid-tier gate may cause users to over-provision.
