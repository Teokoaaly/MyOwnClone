## Claim
Stripe Issuing charges **$0.10 per virtual card** and **$3.50 per standard physical card**, with custom cards subject to additional pricing and shipping rates not included.

## Why It Matters
The low per-virtual-card cost ($0.10) makes mass virtual card issuance feasible for expense management use cases, while the $3.50 physical card cost includes production and shipping within two days. The per-card pricing model scales linearly with volume, unlike percentage-based pricing models that vary with transaction size.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] requires a card upfront for trial conversions, which creates a barrier for new users. Issuing virtual cards at $0.10 each offers a card-on-demand model that bypasses the trial requirement entirely, but the $3.50 physical card cost and 2-day shipping still require commitment — making virtual cards the low-friction entry point for expense management use cases.
