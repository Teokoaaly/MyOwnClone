## Claim
Amazon SES offers three pricing plans (Essentials, Pro, Enterprise) with per-1,000-email rates that vary by volume tier: Essentials ($0.16/1K for 0–10M, $0.14/1K for 10–100M, $0.11/1K for >100M), Pro ($0.22/1K, $0.17/1K, $0.12/1K), and Enterprise ($0.23/1K, $0.18/1K, $0.13/1K).

## Why It Matters
The plan choice determines the per-thousand cost across three volume tiers, directly affecting total monthly spend at scale. Customers can upgrade or switch to à la carte pricing at any time, but the plan sets the baseline rate structure.
