## Claim
OpenAI may grant case-by-case exceptions to certain restrictions for approved partners and uses, particularly for national security, intelligence, and law enforcement uses that benefit people, strengthen democratic governance, and preserve human judgment and accountability. Customers must comply with requirements on preserving human judgment over high-stakes decisions including use of force, prevent mass domestic surveillance, evasion of legal obligations, oversight, or accountability. Model-access intermediaries must obtain prior review and approval from OpenAI.

## Why It Matters
Approved exceptions allow societally beneficial uses of AI (e.g., national security partnerships) that would otherwise be restricted, but they come with strict conditions to preserve human accountability and prevent legal violations. Model-access intermediaries must navigate these requirements to operate lawfully.
