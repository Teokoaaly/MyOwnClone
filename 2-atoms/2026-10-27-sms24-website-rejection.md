## Claim
Some websites reject public or previously used phone numbers, and SMS24.me advises keeping sensitive accounts on a number you control.

## Why It Matters
When a form requires a US, Canadian, or UK number, users must match the country code and enter the number in the format requested by the sender. Shared numbers are not suitable for banking, password recovery, private conversations, or any account where exclusivity of the number is required. For routine browsing, the full number directory and country pages help narrow the choice before requesting an SMS.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-27-free-sms-receive-services]] asserts that Free-SMS-Receive supports SMS verification codes from major platforms including Facebook, Telegram, WeChat, VK, PayPal, and Alipay, among others. Free-SMS-Receive supports a set of named platforms, but individual services may still reject public numbers. Both services note that some websites reject public or previously used numbers, but SMS24.me is more explicit about the limitation: it advises keeping sensitive accounts on a number you control and warns that delivery and acceptance depend on the sending service. Both can be correct — platform support lists are not guarantees of successful registration, and users should test delivery with the target service before relying on it for critical accounts.
