## Claim

The Gologin Business plan costs $99/month for 500 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

At $99/mo for 500 profiles, the price-per-profile further decreases to $0.198, continuing the volume discount trend from the 300-profile tier. This tier enables larger-scale operations where the per-profile cost becomes increasingly competitive compared to alternative antidetect browser solutions or VPS setups.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-business-300-49.50]] notes 300 profiles for $49.50/mo. The price-per-profile improves from $0.165 to $0.198 when moving from 300 to 500 profiles — a counterintuitive increase in cost per profile due to the tier jump. Both statements are factually correct within their respective tier boundaries, yet the user must decide whether the additional 200 profiles are worth the 19.8% increase in cost per profile.
