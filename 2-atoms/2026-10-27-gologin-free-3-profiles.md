## Claim

Gologin offers a free plan including 3 browser profiles with full platform benefits, excluding profile sharing and team member features.

## Why It Matters

The free plan at 3 profiles allows users to trial Gologin's core functionality without financial commitment. This lowers the entry barrier significantly, enabling users to evaluate the fingerprint quality, profile isolation, and browser features before committing to a paid tier. For occasional users or those with minimal isolation needs, the free plan may be sufficient without requiring a paid subscription.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-professional-10-4.50]] notes 10 profiles for $4.50/mo. The free plan offers 3 profiles at $0/mo, but excludes profile sharing and team member features available in paid tiers. A user needing profile sharing must upgrade to a paid plan, creating a functional constraint that the free plan cannot satisfy. Both can be correct — the free plan meets basic isolation needs, while the Professional tier is required for sharing capabilities — but the user's specific workflow requirements determine which tier is appropriate.
