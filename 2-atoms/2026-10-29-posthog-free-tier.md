## Claim
97% of companies use PostHog for free. Full monthly free tier on every product, resets every month, with unlimited team members and 1 project with 1-year data retention.

## Why It Matters
The free tier is the primary user acquisition engine — it removes friction for teams to start using PostHog without a credit card, and the monthly reset means usage limits are predictable and non-threatening. This aligns the free experience with the paid experience so users know exactly what they're getting.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-10-29-posthog-per-event-pricing]] asserts per-event pricing applies after free limits, but the free tier's "usage stops at the free tier limits, so you can't be charged by surprise" means most users never encounter paid pricing. Both can be correct — the free tier is the entry point, and paid pricing only activates when limits are exceeded.
