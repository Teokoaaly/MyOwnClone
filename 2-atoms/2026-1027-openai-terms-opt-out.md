## Claim
Users may opt out of allowing OpenAI to use their Content to train models, though this may limit the ability of Services to better address their specific use case.

## Why It Matters
Users who want to prevent their data from contributing to model improvement have a formal opt-out mechanism, at the potential cost of reduced service personalization and performance.
