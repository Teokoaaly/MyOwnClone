## Claim

MoreLogin provides real canvas fingerprint as a security and antidetect browser feature, available across all pricing tiers including the Free plan, to mask browser fingerprinting and maintain consistent fingerprint presentation across sessions.

## Why It Matters

Real canvas fingerprint masking is a core differentiator for antidetect browsers, as canvas fingerprinting is a prevalent tracking technique; having this feature available from the Free tier lowers the barrier for users who need basic fingerprint protection without paying for higher plans.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-pro-plan]] also includes real canvas fingerprint, but the Pro plan's "customized profile purchase" and "24/7 online support" create a two-tiered access model where advanced fingerprint customization may be restricted to paid features, contradicting the claim that it's uniformly available — or the feature is equally available but the support tier gap creates practical inequity between Free and Pro users.
