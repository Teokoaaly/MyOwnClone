## Claim
The xAI Text API prices per 1M tokens vary by model, with grok-4.7 costing $2.00/$6.00 input/output, grok-4.6 matching grok-4.7 rates, grok-4.5 at $2.00/$12.00, grok-4.3 at $1.25/$5.00, and the grok-4.20 multi-agent/reasoning/non-reasoning variants at $1.25/$5.00; long-context billing applies above the model's threshold, and US regional endpoint requests incur a 1.1x premium over global rates.

## Why It Matters
This is the foundational pricing layer for all xAI text interactions. The granular model differentiation (4.7 > 4.6 > 4.5 > 4.3 > 4.20) lets users trade off capability vs cost, while the 1.1x US regional premium and long-context threshold billing affect total spend predictability. Compared to OpenAI's per-model granularity, xAI consolidates reasoning and non-reasoning under fewer pricing tiers, simplifying budgeting but offering less fine-grained control.
