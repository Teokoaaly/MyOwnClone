## Claim
Data retention: events and metadata guaranteed 7 years on any paid plan, 1 year on free plan. Recordings 1 month free, 90 days pay-as-you-go, up to 1 year with Boost/Scale add-ons, 5 years Enterprise. Logs 14-day default, custom retention $0.05/GB/month.

## Why It Matters
Retention guarantees create trust — users know data won't disappear arbitrarily. The 7-year guarantee on paid plans is competitive, while the 1-year free tier retention is generous compared to competitors. Custom retention billing ($0.05/GB/month) means long-term storage has an explicit cost, encouraging users to be intentional about what they keep.
