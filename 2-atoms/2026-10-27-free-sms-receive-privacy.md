## Claim
Free-SMS-Receive does not collect personal data and retains text messages for no more than 24 hours, protecting user privacy by discarding verification codes after a day.

## Why It Matters
Users avoiding long-term phone number exposure can use this service with the confidence that their verification codes won't persist indefinitely, reducing risk of future reuse or data leakage. The 24-hour window balances usability (enough time to complete verification) with privacy (automatic cleanup).
