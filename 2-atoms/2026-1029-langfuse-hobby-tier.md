## Claim
The Langfuse Hobby tier is free and designed for hobby projects and POCs, requiring no credit card and including 50k units/month with all platform features and 30-day data access.

## Why It Matters
The free entry point lowers the barrier for experimenting with LLM observability, making it accessible for developers and teams to evaluate the platform before committing to a paid plan. This aligns with the broader trend of freemium models in developer tools, where the Hobby tier serves as a gateway to Core and Pro tiers as usage scales.

## [FRICTION]
The Hobby tier includes "All platform features (with limits)" yet caps users at 2 and limits data access to 30 days, while Core offers unlimited users and 90-day access at $29/month. This creates a significant feature wall — the technical capacity is identical but the practical constraints (user count, data retention) force an early upgrade path that may disrupt small teams or side projects mid-stream.
