## Claim

SMS-MAN offers two distinct rental models: one-time numbers valid for up to 20 minutes and rental numbers valid for up to 90 days with unlimited SMS receipt.

## Why It Matters

The one-time number model (up to 20 minutes, $0.05 per activation) suits users who need a single verification code, while the rental model (up to 90 days, unlimited SMS) serves professionals and bulk registrants who need ongoing access to messages. This dual-model approach accommodates both casual users verifying a few accounts and power users managing multiple registrations across services with different verification timelines. The rental model's 16 available countries and "all possible services" coverage contrasts with the one-time model's 200+ countries and 1000+ services, creating a trade-off between breadth of coverage and duration of access.

> [[2026-10-27-free-sms-receive-services]] asserts that Free-SMS-Receive supports one-time SMS verification without specifying rental duration, whereas SMS-MAN explicitly distinguishes between one-time (20 min) and rental (90 day) models with different country and service coverage.
