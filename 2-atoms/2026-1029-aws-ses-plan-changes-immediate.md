## Claim
Plan upgrades take effect immediately when the request is submitted; if a customer was defaulted to Essentials and cancels to à la carte, the downgrade also takes effect immediately. All other downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Why It Matters
Immediate effect means customers can change pricing strategies without waiting for a billing cycle boundary, supporting agile cost management. However, the exception for defaulted Essentials→à la carte means some transition paths have different timing.
