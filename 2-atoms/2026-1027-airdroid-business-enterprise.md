## Claim
AirDroid Business Enterprise costs $33 per device/year and provides a Full MDM Suite with Enterprise-level Service features.

## Why It Matters
At $33/device/year, AirDroid Business Enterprise undercuts many enterprise MDM competitors (such as Jamf and VMware Workspace One) which often charge $50-$150+ per device/year, making it a cost-effective choice for organizations requiring full device management, compliance, and security features at scale.

## [FRICTION]
> Despite being priced at $33/device/year, the Enterprise tier mentions "Contact Sales for a custom quote" for On-Premises Deployment, suggesting the listed price may not include all enterprise features and that actual costs could be significantly higher depending on organizational size and requirements.
