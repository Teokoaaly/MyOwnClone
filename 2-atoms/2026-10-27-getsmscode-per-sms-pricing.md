## Claim

Getsmscode operates on a pay-per-SMS model where users pay per individual verification code received, rather than subscribing to a bundle or monthly plan.

## Why It Matters

The pay-per-SMS model lowers the entry cost for users who only need a single verification code, making it accessible for occasional account creation or OTP receipt. This contrasts with services that require minimum topups or subscriptions, and it benefits users who prioritize cost efficiency over bulk number provisioning. However, per-SMS costs can add up at scale compared to bundled plans.
