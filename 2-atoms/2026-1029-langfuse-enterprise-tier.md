## Claim
The Langfuse Enterprise tier at $2,499/month is designed for large-scale teams, offering everything in Pro + Teams, plus audit logs, SCIM API, custom rate limits, uptime SLA, named lead support engineer, and yearly commitment options with custom volume pricing, AWS Marketplace billing, and invoice-based payment.

## Why It Matters
Enterprise provides the highest level of operational governance, security, and support fidelity for organizations with demanding requirements around compliance, user provisioning, and dedicated support. The yearly commitment pathway (custom volume pricing, AWS Marketplace, invoice billing) aligns with enterprise procurement cycles and can reduce effective cost at very high usage volumes.

## [FRICTION]
Enterprise adds audit logs, SCIM API, custom rate limits, uptime SLA, and a named lead support engineer at $2,499/month with yearly commitment, while Pro at $199/month adds SOC2/ISO27001 and HIPAA BAA but lacks these operational governance features. Teams that need compliance (available at Pro) may still find themselves needing Enterprise for complete operational governance (audit logs, SCIM, SLA), creating a costly tier jump where compliance and operational requirements diverge in their tier placement.
