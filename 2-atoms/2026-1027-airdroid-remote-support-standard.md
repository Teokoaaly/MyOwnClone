## Claim
AirDroid Remote Support Standard costs $599 per license/year and provides Advanced Remote Control Features and Troubleshooting Focus features, best for Unattended Android Devices.

## Why It Matters
At $599/license/year, AirDroid Remote Support Standard targets power users and organizations needing advanced remote control and troubleshooting capabilities for unattended Android devices, representing a significant investment compared to the Basic tier at $199 but offering features like augmented reality camera integration and remote monitoring that justify the premium for technical teams.

## [FRICTION]
> The $599/license/year Standard tier is marketed as a "Limited-time Offer," yet the pricing page does not indicate when this offer expires or what the regular price will be, creating uncertainty for budget planning and potentially locking in customers at a rate that may increase substantially in the future.
