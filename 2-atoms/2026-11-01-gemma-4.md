## Claim
Gemma 4 is Google's lightweight, state-of-the-art open model built from the same technology that powers Gemini models. It serves as the open counterpart to Gemini's closed models.

## Pricing
- Input price: Free of charge; Not available (Free Tier)
- Output price: Free of charge; Not available (Free Tier)
- Context caching price: Free of charge; Not available
- Context caching (storage): Free of charge; Not available
- Tuning price: Not available; Not available
- Grounding with Google Search: Not available; Not available
- Used to improve products: Yes; output: No

## Why It Matters
Gemma 4 provides access to model technology at no cost, suitable for research, distillation, and deployment scenarios where Gemini's licensing or cost is a barrier. Being open and free-tier eligible, it lowers the entry barrier for developers experimenting with Gemini-class capabilities. The "not available" pricing across the board suggests it may be in early access, research preview, or priced through a different channel (e.g., research program, direct partnership).

## [FRICTION]
> The "Not available" pricing across all dimensions creates a coordination friction: developers cannot model or budget Gemma 4 costs using the same framework as Gemini models. Cost estimation requires checking separate documentation or program enrollment, delaying integration planning for teams that need predictable pricing before adoption.
