## Claim
AirDroid Remote Support Basic costs $199 per license/year and provides Remote Assistance Support to Customers and Employees features, best for Unattended Android Devices.

## Why It Matters
At $199/license/year, AirDroid Remote Support Basic positions itself as a mid-tier remote support solution for businesses needing unattended Android device management, undercutting traditional remote support software that often charges $500-$1000+ per license/year for similar unattended device capabilities.

## [FRICTION]
> The $199/license/year price point is specifically marketed for "Unattended Android Devices," yet the pricing page does not clarify whether this license covers a single device or multiple devices, creating uncertainty for organizations with fleets of Android devices needing unattended support.
