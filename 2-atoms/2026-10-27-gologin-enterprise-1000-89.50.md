## Claim

The Gologin Enterprise plan costs $89.50/month for 1000 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

At $89.50/mo for 1000 profiles, the price-per-profile drops to $0.0895, representing a major cost reduction per profile compared to lower tiers. This makes enterprise-scale profile management financially viable for large organizations that need thousands of isolated browser profiles for operations like ad verification, market research, or large-scale account management.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-business-500-99]] notes 500 profiles for $99/mo. Despite the lower price-per-profile of $0.0895 at the Enterprise tier, the total monthly cost of $89.50 for 1000 profiles exceeds the $99 cost for 500 profiles at the Business tier. A user needing exactly 500 profiles would pay more in total at the Enterprise tier, even though the per-profile cost is lower — the friction is between total cost and cost-per-profile optimization.
