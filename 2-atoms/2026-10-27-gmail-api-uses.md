## Claim
The Gmail API is suitable for various applications including read-only mail extraction, indexing, and backup, automated or programmatic message sending, email account migration, email organization including filtering and sorting of messages, and standardization of email signatures across an organization.

## Why It Matters
Identifying the full range of valid use cases helps developers determine whether the Gmail API meets their specific requirements, avoiding over-engineering or selecting an unsuitable email solution.
