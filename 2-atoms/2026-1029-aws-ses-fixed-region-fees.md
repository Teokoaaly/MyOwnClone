## Claim
Pro plan includes a $105 per-account-per-region monthly fixed charge; Enterprise plan includes a $500 per-account-per-region monthly fixed charge, on top of the per-1,000-email rates.

## Why It Matters
These fixed fees represent a significant baseline cost for Pro and Enterprise plans, especially at lower volumes. A Pro user sending only 1M emails pays $105 + $220 = $325, while the same volume on Essentials pays only the variable $160.
