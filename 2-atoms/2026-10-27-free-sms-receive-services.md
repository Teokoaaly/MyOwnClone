## Claim
Free-SMS-Receive supports SMS verification codes from major platforms including Facebook, Telegram, WeChat, VK, PayPal, and Alipay, among others.

## Why It Matters
Users verifying accounts on these platforms can use the service without needing their personal phone number. The breadth of supported services makes it useful for international users who may face regional restrictions with other free SMS services.
