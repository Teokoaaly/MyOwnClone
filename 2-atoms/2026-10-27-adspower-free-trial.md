## Claim
AdsPower offers a 7-day free trial that allows users to explore all features before committing to a paid plan, in addition to the permanently free 2-profile tier.

## Why It Matters
The 7-day free trial provides a risk-free window for users to test the full range of AdsPower features beyond the limited 2-profile Free plan. This trial period lets users experience the Professional plan's capabilities (automation tools, team collaboration, local API) before deciding whether to upgrade. Combined with the permanently free 2 profiles, AdsPower offers two distinct entry points: an immediate free tier for light users and a trial period for serious evaluation before purchase.
