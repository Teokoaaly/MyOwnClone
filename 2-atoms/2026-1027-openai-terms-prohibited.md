## Claim
Users may not use OpenAI Services for illegal, harmful, or abusive activity, including reverse engineering, data extraction, using Output to develop competing models, or representing Output as human-generated.

## Why It Matters
Compliance boundaries define the legitimate use scope. Violations can result in account suspension or termination. The prohibition on using Output to develop competing models is especially relevant for entrepreneurs and researchers.
