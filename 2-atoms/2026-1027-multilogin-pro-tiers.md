## Claim
Multilogin Pro plans tier at three profile limits: Pro 10 ($7.08/mo annual, $12.42/mo monthly), Pro 20 ($19.00/mo monthly, $19.17/mo annual), and Pro 50 ($29.00/mo monthly, $29.92/mo annual). All Pro plans include monthly premium proxy traffic, mobile minutes allowances, team seats (1-2), API access, automation-friendly features, profile transfer, templates, bulk operations, and Cookie Robot. The profile limit is the primary differentiator within Pro tiers.

## Why It Matters
Profile limit is the main axis of differentiation for Pro plans. Users who need automation or team collaboration need API access (included in all Pro plans) and team seats. The price jump from Pro 10 to Pro 20 is $6.58/mo monthly or $14.92/yr annually — users should match the profile limit to actual account needs rather than over-provisioning.

## [FRICTION]
> The Pro 10 annual price ($7.08/mo) is effectively the same as the monthly minimum entry point for all paid plans, but monthly billing costs $12.42/mo — a 75% premium for monthly vs annual. Users on monthly billing pay significantly more over time. The annual 35% discount is a strong incentive but requires upfront commitment.

> API access is included in all Pro plans but the Free plan has no API access. Users starting on Free who later need automation must upgrade to Pro, losing the zero-credit-card-onboarding they started with.
