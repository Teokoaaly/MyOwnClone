## Claim
cto.new's Guide Shop allows users to turn their expertise on a topic into illustrated guides that can be sold, transforming knowledge into a monetizable product without needing design or publishing skills.

## Why It Matters
Enabling subject-matter experts to package and sell their knowledge as guides lowers the barrier to monetization for content creators and complements the platform's AI team features by providing a tangible deliverable.
