## Claim

Sentry's Seer: AI debugging agent is an add-on subscription available at additional cost on Team and Business tiers. Annual billing offers a "Save with annual" tag but the exact discount percentage is not disclosed on the pricing page, requiring users to proceed to the signup flow to see the annual vs. monthly rate split.

## Why It Matters

Seer is listed as a subscription add-on under the Team tier ($26/mo) and Business tier ($80/mo), suggesting it may be priced per-user, per-organization, or as a separate module. The annual discount tag implies cost savings but the lack of transparent rate splitting creates friction for budget-conscious teams choosing between monthly flexibility and annual commitment.

## [FRICTION]

> The pricing page displays **$26/mo** and **$80/mo** for Team and Business respectively, with a "Save with annual" tag beneath, but does not state the annual total, monthly equivalent, or discount percentage. Teams budgeting annually cannot compare Sentry's cost against monthly-priced competitors without initiating signup.

> **Seer: AI Debugging Agent** appears as a line item under both Team and Business tiers with the note "subscription required," but there is no per-price, per-seat, or consumption-based rate disclosed on the pricing page. Users must navigate to the product page or signup flow to discover Seer's cost, creating a discoverability friction.

> The annual billing discount is tagged but not quantified, which means teams cannot calculate total cost of ownership upfront. This may pressure users into committing to annual plans without full visibility, or conversely, cause monthly-planning teams to overlook Sentry if they require transparent per-month pricing.

> The Developer tier is free but limited to one user, with no mention of Seer availability at any price point, leaving solo developers uncertain whether Seer can be added individually or if the tier restriction is absolute.
