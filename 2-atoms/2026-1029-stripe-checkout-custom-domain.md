## Claim
Stripe Checkout custom domain costs **$10.00 per month** plus **0.4% of the transaction total**, capped at **$2.00 per invoice**, enabling businesses to use their own domain while hosting the payments page on Stripe Checkout.

## Why It Matters
The 0.4% per-transaction fee on top of the base payments pricing (2.9% + 30¢) provides a way to offset custom domain costs for high-volume businesses — the $2.00 cap ensures the fee remains predictable for low-value transactions, while high-volume merchants pay a capped maximum per invoice.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] requires a card upfront for trial conversions, which creates self-service onboarding friction. The Checkout custom domain lowers friction for repeat customers with a branded experience, but the trial card requirement remains the primary onboarding barrier — the custom domain fee does not mitigate the upfront card hurdle.
