## Claim
Forwarding addresses are specific, verified email addresses configured to receive auto-forwarded messages from a user's account; before using an address in a forwarding rule or filter, it must be registered and verified to prove ownership.

## Why It Matters
Verified forwarding addresses ensure that auto-forwarded mail reaches the intended destination and prevent unauthorized forwarding, which is critical for compliance and security in Google Workspace deployments.
