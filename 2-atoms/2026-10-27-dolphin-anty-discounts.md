## Claim
Dolphin{anty} offers volume discounts: 20% off for 6-11 month commitments and 40% off for annual (12 month) commitments.

## Why It Matters
The discount structure incentivizes long-term commitment, with the 40% annual discount effectively reducing the Base plan from $89 to ~$53.40/month, making Enterprise more accessible and encouraging users to lock in annual spending rather than month-to-month flexibility.
