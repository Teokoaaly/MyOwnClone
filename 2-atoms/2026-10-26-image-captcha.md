## Claim

Image captcha costs $0.50–$1.00 per 1,000 solves with a 1-second solving speed and 12,000 free capacity per minute.

## Why It Matters

Image captcha shares the same price range and solving speed as normal captcha, but is distinguished by its visual puzzle format. The 12,000 free capacity per minute is the highest among all captcha types in the table, making it the most generous free tier. This combination of low cost, fastest speed, and highest free capacity makes image captcha the optimal choice for bulk solving workloads where captcha type is not a differentiating factor.
