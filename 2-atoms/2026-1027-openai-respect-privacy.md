## Claim
OpenAI prohibits compromising others' privacy: aggregating, monitoring, profiling, or distributing private/sensitive information without authorization; facial recognition databases without data subject consent; real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces; using someone's likeness (photorealistic image or voice) without consent in ways that could confuse authenticity; evaluation or classification based on social behavior, personal traits, or biometric data (social scoring, profiling, inferring sensitive attributes); and inferring emotions in workplace/educational settings except for medical or safety reasons.

## Why It Matters
Privacy prohibitions prevent mass surveillance, social scoring, and pervasive profiling enabled by AI. They protect individuals from having their data exploited at scale and set boundaries on how behavioral and biometric data may be inferred or used in employment, education, and other sensitive contexts.
