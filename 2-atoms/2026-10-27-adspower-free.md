## Claim
The Free plan offers 2 profiles forever at $0 cost, with no time limit or trial period.

## Why It Matters
The Free plan serves as the entry point for new users to experience AdsPower's antidetect browser capabilities without financial commitment. With 2 free profiles, users can test the core functionality before deciding to upgrade, making it a low-risk onboarding mechanism for individuals or small-scale operations.
