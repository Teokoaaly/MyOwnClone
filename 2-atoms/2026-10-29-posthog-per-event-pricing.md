## Claim
Per-event pricing: first 1M events free per month per product, identified events $0.000198 each after, anonymous $0.00005 each after. Group analytics add-on $0.000071 per identified event. Pricing per product resets monthly with billing limits to cap surprise charges.

## Why It Matters
Usage-based pricing means you only pay for what you capture — no per-user seat fees, no minimum seats. The gradient from free (1M events) to pay-per-use ($0.000198/identified event) creates a low barrier for growth, but costs scale with volume, so teams must monitor event volume to avoid unexpected charges.
