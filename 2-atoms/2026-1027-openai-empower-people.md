## Claim
OpenAI prohibits using services for academic dishonesty; deceit, fraud, scams, spam, or impersonation; political campaigning, lobbying, foreign/domestic election interference, or demobilization; and automation of high-stakes actions in sensitive areas (critical infrastructure, education, housing, employment, financial activities/credit, insurance, legal, medical, essential government services, product safety components) without human review.

## Why It Matters
These prohibitions prevent manipulation, deception, and interference with human rights and critical services. They ensure AI is not used to undermine democratic processes, exploit vulnerabilities, or automate consequential decisions that materially affect rights, safety, and access to essential services without appropriate human oversight.
