## Claim

MoreLogin provides batch operations for creating, deleting, launching, and importing profiles, cookies, and proxies — with yes/yes comparison indicators across all feature categories, meaning batch functionality is universally available across Free and Pro plans.

## Why It Matters

Batch operations dramatically reduce administrative overhead for users managing multiple accounts, enabling bulk profile creation, cookie import, proxy import, and profile launch in a single action — this is particularly valuable for e-commerce, marketing, and automation workflows where individual profile management would be prohibitively time-consuming.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-free-plan]] lists batch operations as included features, but the Free plan's 2-profile limit means batch creation of 2 profiles is functionally useful, while batch operations targeting 10+ profiles (as the Pro plan supports) remain inaccessible at the Free tier — the yes/yes comparison flags are technically accurate but practically misleading given the profile cap, creating a disconnect between feature availability and actual usability.
