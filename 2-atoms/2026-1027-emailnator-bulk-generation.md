## Claim
Emailnator supports generating up to 100 temporary Gmail addresses at once, useful for QA, testing, or bulk signups where multiple disposable inboxes are needed simultaneously.

## Why It Matters
Every address is live the moment it's generated with no confirmation email, no CAPTCHA, and no queue to wait in. This scales disposable email from individual signups to batch operations, saving significant time in testing workflows.
