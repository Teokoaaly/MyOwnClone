## Claim
Multilogin pricing is offered in two billing cycles: monthly and annual. Annual billing provides a 35% discount compared to monthly pricing. All plans (Free, Pro, Business) are available on either cycle. Monthly billing costs more but offers flexibility to upgrade/downgrade each cycle with prorated billing. Annual billing commits to one year with the discounted rate; upgrades during the year prorate the remaining value toward the new plan, and if the new plan costs less, the difference is returned as Multipoints credit.

## Why It Matters
The 35% annual discount is a significant cost saving for committed users. The prorated upgrade/downgrade mechanism reduces risk when changing plans mid-cycle. Multipoints credit for downgrades creates a soft landing — users don't lose value when reducing their plan.

## [FRICTION]
> Users on monthly billing pay a 75% premium annually versus the annual discounted rate. Over a year, a Pro 10 plan costs $149.04 monthly vs $85.00 annually — a $64.04 difference (43% savings with annual). Users who optimize for flexibility without evaluating annual commitment can effectively double their Multilogin costs.

> Prorated billing works differently for upgrades vs downgrades. Upgrades immediately apply the remaining subscription value toward the new plan. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle (not immediately), meaning users stay at the higher tier until the cycle ends. Users needing to reduce costs quickly have less flexibility than expected.

> The Free plan has no credit card requirement and no time limit, but users who start on Free monthly and later upgrade annually may face a larger commitment jump than users who start on Pro annual from the beginning.
