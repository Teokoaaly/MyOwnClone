## Claim
The Langfuse Pro tier at $199/month is designed for scaling projects, offering 100k units/month included, 3-year data access, data retention management, unlimited annotation queues, high rate limits, SOC2 & ISO27001 reports, BAA/HIPAA availability, and an optional Teams add-on for $300/month with enterprise SSO, RBAC, and dedicated Slack support.

## Why It Matters
Pro adds compliance and governance features (SOC2, ISO27001, HIPAA BAA) and extended data retention (3 years vs 90 days) that production teams in regulated industries require. The optional Teams add-on unlockes enterprise-grade administration (SSO, RBAC, dedicated support) without requiring the full Enterprise tier, making it a flexible midpoint for teams needing administrative control and compliance certification.

## [FRICTION]
Pro adds SOC2/ISO27001 and HIPAA BAA at $199/month, while Enterprise at $2,499/month adds the same compliance plus audit logs, SCIM API, custom rate limits, uptime SLA, and a named lead support engineer. The compliance features are available at the Pro tier, but the operational and SLA features that complete the enterprise picture are only at Enterprise, creating a tier jump where teams needing compliance may still need to upgrade to Enterprise for full operational governance.
