## Claim
Stripe Payment Links offer a **custom domain option for $10.00 per month**, allowing businesses to use their own domain while hosting the payments page on Stripe Checkout.

## Why It Matters
The $10/month custom domain fee on Payment Links adds a predictable overhead for businesses that want branded checkout experiences without building their own payment page infrastructure — an alternative to the $10/month custom domain fee on Checkout, which also includes 0.4% per transaction with a $2.00 cap per invoice.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] establishes that trial conversions require a card upfront, which creates friction for self-service onboarding. A custom domain on Payment Links reduces friction for returning customers who recognize the domain, but the upfront card requirement in trials remains a separate barrier that the custom domain does not address.
