## Claim

The Pro plan costs $5.4/month (40% off the regular $9/month) and includes 10 profiles and 10 users, providing all Free plan features plus no limits on profile purchase, customized profile purchase, 24/7 online support, and access to all functions including custom profiles, custom teams, and dedicated consulting service.

## Why It Matters

At $5.4/month with 40% off, the Pro plan reduces the price-per-profile compared to the Free tier's 2-profile limit, making it the recommended entry point for users needing more than 2 profiles while maintaining cost efficiency for solo users or small teams requiring expanded profile capacity.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-free-plan]] claims the Free plan includes "powerful team collaboration." The Pro plan's 10-profile limit with 10 users still constrains team size — if a team needs 15+ profiles, the Pro plan's 10-user ceiling creates a hard boundary that contradicts the notion of "powerful" collaboration, marking this for human judgment rather than auto-resolution.
