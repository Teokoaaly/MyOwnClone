## Claim
All disputes must be resolved through mandatory binding arbitration with a sole arbitrator in California, class and jury trial rights are waived, and users may opt out of arbitration or arbitration term changes within 30 days of account creation or update.

## Why It Matters
Users waive the right to class actions or jury trials, forcing individual arbitration even for widespread issues. The 30-day opt-out window means timely action is required to preserve trial rights.
