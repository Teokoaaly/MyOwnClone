## Claim
AdsPower distinguishes between 'number of users' (team members managed by a super-administrator) and 'number of profiles' (unique browser fingerprint environments), as defined in the FAQ section.

## Why It Matters
Understanding this distinction is critical for planning team workflows. 'Users' refers to human team members with assigned permissions, while 'profiles' refers to independent browser environments. A single user might manage multiple profiles, and multiple users can share profiles with different permission levels. This separation determines how teams structure their AdsPower accounts and assign access controls, rather than conflating team size with browser instance count.
