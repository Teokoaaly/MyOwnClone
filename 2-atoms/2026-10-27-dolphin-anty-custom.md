## Claim
Dolphin{anty} offers custom plans with unlimited browser profiles and personalized pricing per 100/1000 profiles, along with unlimited users for enterprise-scale needs.

## Why It Matters
Custom plans remove the profile and user caps of fixed tiers, allowing organizations with unique scaling requirements to negotiate tailored pricing, though at a higher complexity cost due to bespoke negotiation rather than self-service signup.
