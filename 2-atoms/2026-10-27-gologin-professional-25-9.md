## Claim

The Gologin Professional plan costs $9/month for 25 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

At $9/mo for 25 profiles, the price-per-profile drops to $0.36, making this tier more cost-effective per profile than the 10-profile tier at $0.45/profile. Users needing more than 10 profiles benefit from the volume discount, which is favorable for small-scale operations or freelancers managing multiple accounts.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-professional-10-4.50]] notes 10 profiles for $4.50/mo. The price-per-profile improves from $0.45 to $0.36 when upgrading to 25 profiles, creating a pricing incentive to upgrade that may not align with the user's actual needs. Both cannot be simultaneously optimal — the user must judge whether the additional 15 profiles justify the change in cost structure.
