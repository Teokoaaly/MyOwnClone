## Claim
Multi-product benefit: usage across any of PostHog Web, Slack, Desktop, or MCP counts toward combined organization-wide usage and free allowances. Billing is organization-wide so all projects combine into one bill with one free allowance. Adding projects doesn't add extra free usage — the free plan includes one project, paid plans include more.

## Why It Matters
Organizations can consolidate tools under one roof — using PostHog for analytics, session replay, feature flags, and workflows under a single bill simplifies procurement and gives better spend visibility. The tradeoff is that a spike in one product consumes from the shared free allowance, so teams must monitor cross-product usage.
