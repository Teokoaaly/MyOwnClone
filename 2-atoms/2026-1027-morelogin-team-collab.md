## Claim

MoreLogin offers powerful team collaboration features including profile data cloud synchronization, account password protection, operation logs, flexible permission management, and batch operations for creating, deleting, launching, and importing profiles, cookies, and proxies — all available across Free and Pro plans.

## Why It Matters

Team collaboration features enable multiple users to manage shared browser profiles securely, with granular permissions and audit logs, making MoreLogin suitable for teams of any size rather than just individual users — the synchronizer, permission management, and batch operations collectively transform a solo browser into a team workflow platform.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-free-plan]] includes "powerful team collaboration" in its feature list, but the Free plan limits to 2 profiles and 2 users, which fundamentally constrains what "powerful" collaboration can mean at that scale — the synchronizer and permission features may work technically with 2 profiles, but they don't constitute "powerful" team collaboration in any practical sense, marking this claim for human judgment on the gap between marketing language and structural limits.
