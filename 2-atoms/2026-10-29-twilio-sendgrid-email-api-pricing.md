## Twilio SendGrid Email API Pricing

### Plans

- **Free trial**: $0/mo for 60 days, 100 emails/day, no credit card required
- **Essentials**: Starting $19.95/mo, 50K–100K emails/mo
- **Pro**: Starting $89.95/mo, 100K–2.5M emails/mo, includes dedicated IPs, subuser management, SSO, email validation
- **Premier**: Custom pricing, high-volume customization, contact sales

### Key Ideas

- Volume discounts apply as you scale beyond included thresholds
- Feature gap: dedicated IPs, SSO, and email testing are Pro-tier and above
- Overage charges may apply if you exceed plan limits; upgrading usually beats overages
- Free trial gives full feature access for 60 days with no commitment

### Friction

- **FREE_TRIAL_LIMIT**: 100 emails/day is very low — suitable only for testing, not realistic workload assessment
- **ESSentials_OVERAGE**: Overage charges apply; not clearly priced upfront, making cost prediction difficult at scale
- **PRO_TIER_GAP**: Dedicated IPs, SSO, and email validation are Pro-only; Essentials users must upgrade just for these features
- **PREMIER_UNCERTAINTY**: Custom pricing requires sales contact, creating friction for self-serve budgeting
- **SCALING_COST_JUMP**: Cost increases significantly from Essentials ($19.95) to Pro ($89.95) — a 4.5x jump for the next volume tier
