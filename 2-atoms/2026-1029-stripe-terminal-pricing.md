## Claim
Stripe Terminal pricing is **2.7% + 5¢ per successful transaction for domestic cards** and **1.5% for international cards**, plus **$0.10 per authorization for Tap to Pay** on compatible iPhone or Android devices.

## Why It Matters
Terminal unifies online and in-person payments with a single integration. The domestic rate of 2.7% + 5¢ is slightly lower than the 2.9% + 30¢ standard rate, while the international rate of 1.5% is significantly lower than the 1.5% + 1% currency conversion surcharge, making Terminal attractive for businesses with mixed domestic/in-person sales channels.

## [FRICTION]
> [[2026-1022-stripe-planes-trial-30-dias]] requires a card upfront for trial conversions, which is a self-service onboarding barrier. Terminal's in-person model does not require a card upfront for hardware-based transactions, but the $0.10 Tap to Pay authorization fee adds a per-transaction cost that doesn't exist with traditional card readers, creating a different friction profile for mobile-first businesses.
