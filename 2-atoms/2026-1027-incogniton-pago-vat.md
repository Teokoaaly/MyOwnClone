## Claim

Incogniton accepts payment via **Stripe**, **PayPal**, **credit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), and **cryptocurrencies** (Bitcoin BTC, Ethereum ETH, Litecoin LTC, Dogecoin DOGE, Dai). VAT may apply in certain countries for digital services. Businesses registered for VAT can enter their business number and VAT number to be exempted from the VAT charge, as explained in the account details edit guide.

## Why It Matters

Payment method availability is consistent across all pricing tiers, but VAT treatment creates a cost differential depending on jurisdiction and business registration status. The ability to claim VAT exemption for registered businesses effectively reduces the monthly cost by the VAT percentage (typically 20% in EU countries), making the Custom Package's high baseline price more competitive for enterprise customers. Payment failures are typically attributed to delays, and users are instructed to contact support to activate subscriptions in advance.
