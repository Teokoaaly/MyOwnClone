## Claim

Resend allocates AI credits monthly by plan tier: Free gets 5 credits, Pro gets 100 credits, Scale gets 500 credits, and Enterprise has flexible allocation, with credits used for AI-assisted email features.

## Why It Matters

AI credits are a non-trivial differentiator across plans. The 20x jump from Free (5) to Pro (100) and 5x jump from Pro (100) to Scale (500) means teams with AI-dependent workflows will hit credit limits quickly on lower tiers. A team using AI for email generation or summarization on Pro plan could exhaust 100 credits in depending on usage intensity, then either stop AI features or pay overage (not explicitly priced for credits). The Free plan's 5 credits are largely symbolic, suitable only for occasional AI testing. Teams should model AI feature consumption against plan selection — if AI credits are mission-critical, Scale's 500 credits may be the minimum viable tier, despite the $70/mo jump from Pro.
