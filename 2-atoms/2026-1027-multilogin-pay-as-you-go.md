## Claim
Multilogin offers pay-as-you-go add-ons for additional resources beyond plan allowances. Proxy traffic costs $2.275 per GB (bought in bulk), with unused traffic rolling over monthly. Mobile minutes cost $0.0067 per minute (bought in bulk), with unused minutes rolling over. Add-on packages offer increasing discounts: 5% off at 20,000 min, 8% off at 50,000 min, 11% off at 100,000 min, 14% off at 200,000 min, 17% off at 500,000 min, 20% off at 1,000,000 min.

## Why It Matters
Pay-as-you-go allows users to precisely control costs without upgrading their plan. The rolling-over model means unused resources aren't wasted. Bulk discounts make larger purchases progressively cheaper, encouraging users to buy more upfront if their usage is predictable.

## [FRICTION]
> Pay-as-you-go proxy traffic ($2.275/GB) is premium pricing compared to datacenter proxies from other sources ($1-3/GB range), but Multilogin proxies are integrated and rotation-managed within the platform. Users comparing to external proxy providers may underestimate the convenience premium they're paying.

> Mobile minutes roll over but only within the same billing cycle. Users who skip a month or have highly variable usage may lose minutes if they don't track consumption. The "max 180 minutes per session billed" rule means very long sessions automatically cap billed time, but users must manually stop profiles or enable auto-stop to avoid unexpected charges.

> The smallest mobile minute add-on package is 2,000 minutes at $16.80. For occasional users, this is a large minimum purchase. Per-minute pricing without a smaller entry point can make minute tracking feel restrictive.
