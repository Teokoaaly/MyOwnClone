## Claim

The Gologin Professional plan costs $4.50/month for 10 unique browser profiles.

## Why It Matters

The $4.50/mo entry price point makes Gologin accessible for solo users who need a small number of isolated browser profiles for tasks like account management or testing. This low-cost tier lowers the barrier to entry compared to virtual private servers or other antidetect browser solutions.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-10-27-gologin-professional-25-9]] asserts 25 profiles for $9/mo. Both cannot be the optimal choice for a user with exactly 10 profiles — the price-per-profile drops from $0.45 to $0.36 per profile at the 25-profile tier, creating a pricing incentive to upgrade that may not align with the user's actual needs.
