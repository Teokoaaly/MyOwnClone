## Claim
Free-SMS-Receive is a free service for receiving mobile phone SMS verification codes online without requiring user registration.

## Why It Matters
Eliminating the registration barrier lowers the friction for users who need temporary SMS verification for account creation or OTP receipt. This enables faster onboarding across platforms that require phone verification, particularly for users prioritizing speed over long-term number retention.
