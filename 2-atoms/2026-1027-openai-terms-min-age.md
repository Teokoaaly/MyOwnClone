## Claim
Minimum age to use OpenAI Services is 13 years old; users under 18 must have parent or legal guardian permission.

## Why It Matters
This establishes the baseline eligibility for all OpenAI users and has implications for compliance in educational, family, and minor-focused contexts. Schools and parents must manage consent for minors using the Services.
