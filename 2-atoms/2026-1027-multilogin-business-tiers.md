## Claim
Multilogin Business plans start at Business 100 ($57.08/mo monthly, $685.00/yr annually) and scale to Business 500+ with increasing profile limits, proxy traffic, and mobile minutes. Business plans include unlimited team seats, advanced team management, proxy and profile templates, API access with custom rate limits, and priority support. The profile limit, monthly proxy traffic, and monthly mobile minutes grow with each tier.

## Why It Matters
Business plans are designed for agencies and larger teams needing unlimited collaboration. The key value is unlimited team seats + advanced management + custom API rate limits. Proxy traffic scales from 5 GB (Business 100) upward, and mobile minutes from 150/month upward. Users moving from Pro to Business gain unlimited seats and higher resource ceilings.

## [FRICTION]
> The price jump from Pro 50 ($29.00/mo monthly) to Business 100 ($57.08/mo monthly) is ~97% — nearly double the cost. Users with 50 profiles who don't need unlimited seats or advanced management may overpay by switching to Business solely for a higher profile limit.

> Business plans include unlimited team seats, but the "Advanced team management" feature (roles/permissions, groups, folder access) has no granularity controls visible on the pricing page. Users needing precise permission matrices may find the feature set less controllable than expected.

> Mobile minutes scale from 30 (Free one-time) → 60 (Pro 10) → 75 (Pro 20) → 85 (Pro 50) → 150 (Business 100). The Business 100 jump from Pro 50's 85 min is +65 minutes (+76%), but the price doubles. Users whose main need is mobile minutes may find the pay-as-you-go add-on ($0.0067/min) more cost-effective than upgrading plans.
