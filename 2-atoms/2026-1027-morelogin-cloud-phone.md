## Claim

Cloud Phone uses a flexible pay-per-minute billing model at $0.006/minute, capped at $1.5/day, with a monthly cap of $23-$25/30 days; users only pay when they use it, and a monthly cap helps control operational costs effectively.

## Why It Matters

The pay-per-minute model with daily and monthly caps provides predictable cost control for businesses using Cloud Phone for social media, e-commerce, short video account management, and other scenarios, while only charging for actual usage rather than fixed monthly fees.

## [FRICTION]

> [[2026-1027-morelogin-pro-plan]] claims 24/7 online support as a Pro plan feature. The Cloud Phone billing model's "$23-$25/30 days" monthly cap range introduces ambiguity — if a user consistently hits the $1.5/day cap, the monthly total could exceed $25, creating a pricing discrepancy between the advertised cap range and actual usage-based costs that requires human judgment to reconcile.
