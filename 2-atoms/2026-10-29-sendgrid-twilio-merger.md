## Claim

SendGrid and Twilio have merged their websites into Twilio.com, consolidating all email solutions under one domain while keeping their products, pricing, and support fully supported and separate.

## Why It Matters

After the merger, users can access all SendGrid email products (Free trial, Essentials, Pro, Premier) and Twilio's full platform from a single URL. This simplifies discovery but means pricing and feature details that were previously on sendgrid.com are now under twilio.com. The free trial remains available with no credit card required, and existing SendGrid customers retain full access to their plans and resources.

## [FRICTION]

- **URL_CHANGE_FRICTION**: Existing SendGrid users expecting pricing on sendgrid.com may find the content has moved to twilio.com, creating a minor friction point for self-serve budgeting and plan comparison. The existing atom `[[2026-10-29-twilio-sendgrid-email-api-pricing]]` documents the detailed pricing that is now hosted under the Twilio domain.

- **PREMIER_UNCERTAINTY**: Custom Premier pricing now requires contacting sales through Twilio's sales channel rather than SendGrid's dedicated sales path, which may change the handoff experience for high-volume customers.
