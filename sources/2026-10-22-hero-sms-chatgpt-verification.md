## Why Use a Virtual Number for ChatGPT?

One major advantage of using a virtual phone number service is the ability to receive SMS verification codes for ChatGPT registration from any location — no physical SIM card required. This makes it convenient to create or verify accounts while traveling or living abroad, or in countries where ChatGPT access is restricted.

Virtual numbers also suit those seeking extra privacy: the disposable, temporary number cannot be traced back to your real identity, keeping your personal information separate from your ChatGPT activities. Unlike a physical SIM card, a virtual number works entirely over the internet — no hardware required. Your real number is never exposed, and nothing links the temporary number back to you.

## Security Concerns: Giving OpenAI Your Phone Number

While OpenAI requests phone numbers for authentication and account security, providing your personal number carries risks. Any future data breach could expose your number alongside your usage history. Phone numbers linked to an identity can also be targeted in SIM swap scam attacks or used to facilitate identity theft. Even with OpenAI's commitment not to sell your data, users are right to be cautious.

## FAQ

Can I use a temporary phone number to verify ChatGPT if my country has banned it?

Yes. A virtual number issued from a country where ChatGPT is available lets you complete SMS verification and create an account regardless of your physical location. Pair it with a VPN or proxy server to mask your IP address, and registration proceeds as normal.

Is it safe to use a virtual number for ChatGPT authentication?

Yes, and it is often safer than providing your real telephone number. A temporary number keeps your personal identity separate from your ChatGPT account, eliminating the risk of identity theft or a SIM swap scam in the event of a data breach. Once the verification code is used, the number can be discarded entirely.

What happens if ChatGPT requires a password reset or multi-factor authentication later?

Virtual numbers from HeroSMS are intended for one-time verification at registration. For future account recovery, OpenAI offers email-based alternatives — make sure to save your login credentials and keep your email address accessible.

Does a virtual VoIP number work for ChatGPT verification, or does OpenAI block them?

OpenAI does block some VoIP phone numbers during verification. HeroSMS provides numbers specifically selected to pass ChatGPT's verification checks. If a particular number is rejected, simply release it and select a new one from a different country — the process takes under a minute.
