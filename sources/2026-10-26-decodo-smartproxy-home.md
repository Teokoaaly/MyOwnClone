Yes. Web Data API supports AI-focused workflows with LLM-ready Markdown and structured JSON formats (where applicable), plus integrations with MCP, LangChain, n8n, and OpenClaw. ### Do proxies really provide anonymity?

A proxy masks your device’s IP by routing traffic through another IP. The level of anonymity depends on the proxy and how it’s configured, so not every proxy provides the same level of privacy. Decodo’s proxies are designed to mask your original IP and support location-specific access.

### What is the difference between a proxy server and a VPN?

A proxy routes traffic through another IP, typically at the application or connection level. A VPN routes your device’s traffic through an encrypted tunnel, protecting the data in transit as well as masking your IP. Proxies are generally better suited for tasks that require specific IPs, locations, or per-application control, while VPNs are designed primarily for securing a device’s internet connection.
