## Frequently asked questions

What is Proton Workspace?

View details

Proton Workspace combines all the secure productivity tools you need to run your organization under one account. The plan includes [email, calendar](https://proton.me/business/mail), [password manager](https://proton.me/business/pass), [VPN](https://proton.me/business/vpn), [cloud storage](https://proton.me/business/drive), [video conferencing](https://proton.me/business/meet) and a [private AI](https://proton.me/business/lumo) assistant. From your administrator account, you’ll be able to add and manage users, enforce security settings, allocate storage, and more.

What if I don’t need all the Proton business apps?

View details

You can choose plans specific to your needs. We offer flexible business plans for Proton Mail, Proton VPN, Proton Pass, and Meet. If your team only needs email and calendar capabilities, you can start with Mail Essentials. Otherwise you can select Proton Workspace, which allows administrators to adjust access rights.

[See all our plans.](https://proton.me/business/plans)

Can I use my existing business email if I switch to Proton?

View details

Migrating to Proton is simple. You can keep your email addresses and easily migrate all your emails, calendar events, logins, files, and more. Live support is also available with our Support team.

[Learn how to get started with Proton Mail.](https://proton.me/support/how-to-get-your-business-started-in-proton-mail)

Is Proton more secure than other business solutions?

View details

Unlike Google, Microsoft, and others, we use end-to-end encryption to prevent hackers or anyone else (including us) from accessing your data on our servers.

We also help your team use strong passwords and block attacks at the source. For example, our [Proton Sentinel](https://proton.me/support/proton-sentinel) security program uses AI and human analysts to monitor for and block account takeover attacks.

I have a few specific needs. How can I find out if Proton is right for my business?

View details

Proton is built for any business — from sole proprietors to large enterprises. [Search our support articles](https://proton.me/support/business) for details about our products. Or simply [contact our team](https://proton.me/business/contact?pd=mail&int=demo) and we’ll get back to you quickly.
