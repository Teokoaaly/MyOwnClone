## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a proxy cost?

Proxy Server (datacenter) plans start at $2.99/month for 100 proxies, as low as $0.0179/proxy at scale. Static Residential (ISP) plans start at $6/month for 20 proxies. Rotating Residential starts at $3.50/GB. A free 10-proxy plan is available, no credit card required.

### Is Webshare free?

Yes. Webshare’s free plan includes 10 shared proxies and 1GB of monthly bandwidth, with HTTP and SOCKS5 support. There’s no time limit and no credit card required. When you need more proxies, bandwidth, or a different proxy type, you can upgrade to a paid plan from your dashboard. Paid Proxy Server plans start at $2.99/month.

### How much do residential proxies cost?

It depends on which residential product you need, because they’re priced differently:

- Rotating Residential is priced per GB of bandwidth. It starts at $3.50/GB for a 1GB plan and drops to $1.40/GB at 3,000GB, billed monthly based on the plan size you choose.
- Static Residential (ISP) is priced per proxy. It starts at $6/month for 20 proxies ($0.30/proxy) and drops to $0.225/proxy at 10,000 proxies.

Rotating Residential suits workflows that need a large, rotating IP pool. Static Residential suits workflows that need the same IP over a long session.

### Does Webshare offer discounts?

Yes. Webshare’s discounts are built into its pricing:

- Proxy Server: up to 40% off at volume, from $0.0299/proxy down to $0.0179/proxy at 60,000+ proxies.
- Static Residential (ISP): up to 25% off at volume, down to $0.225/proxy at 10,000 proxies.
- Rotating Residential: the per-GB price drops from $3.50/GB to $1.40/GB on larger plans.
- Yearly billing: save 30% compared with monthly billing.

You can also start with the free plan before you commit to a paid plan.

### What’s the difference between shared, private, and dedicated proxy pricing?

Shared, private, and dedicated are access options, not separate proxy types. They’re available on Proxy Server and Static Residential (ISP) plans:

- Shared proxies are used by multiple customers and are the lowest-cost option. They’re the prices shown on this page.
- Private proxies are shared with 1-2 users and cost more.
- Dedicated proxies are assigned exclusively to you.

See [private proxy pricing](https://www.webshare.io/private-proxy) and [dedicated proxy pricing](https://www.webshare.io/dedicated-proxy).

### Is bandwidth included in my plan?

It depends on the product:

- Proxy Server and Static Residential (ISP) plans include 250GB of bandwidth, and you can upgrade to unlimited bandwidth, subject to the fair usage policy.
- Rotating Residential is priced by bandwidth, so your plan size sets your monthly GB allowance.
- The free plan includes 1GB per month.

### Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time during the month, and you’re only charged the difference. When you upgrade, your existing proxies stay the same and you get new ones added. To cancel, switch to the free plan from your dashboard.

### What payment methods do you accept?

Webshare accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners, China UnionPay, JCB, Cartes Bancaires, and Interac. Google Pay and Apple Pay are also available on mobile devices.

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, if you cancel within 2 days of the original payment and:

- your bandwidth usage is below 1GB on Proxy Server or Static Residential plans, or below 0.1GB on Rotating Residential plans
- you’ve used fewer than 1,000 proxies

To request a refund, contact support@webshare.io. The full policy is in our [Terms of Service](https://www.webshare.io/terms).
