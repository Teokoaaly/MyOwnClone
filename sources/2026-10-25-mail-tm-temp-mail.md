# Temp Mail

A free service that gives you an email address and a password for it the moment you open the site, with no personal details required. The mailbox is created automatically, with no link to your identity, and works for signing up on any website or service. Receive messages and account activation codes without ever revealing your real address, keep your privacy intact, and spare your main inbox the spam and junk mail. You also shield it from phishing, bot attacks and leaked databases: the fewer sites that know your real email, the better. Use a temporary mailbox and forget about spam, marketing blasts and nagging notifications, along with the break-in attempts that come with them. All this with no commitments on your side. You give us no personal data and sign no user agreements: just open the site and use it. We value simplicity, reliability and your time.

Secure

Every temporary mailbox is protected by a unique, automatically generated password. Nobody but you can see what's inside: not us, not other users, not would-be attackers. The mailbox itself is kept forever; or delete it yourself, if that gives you more peace of mind.

Simple

Creating a temporary mailbox takes no effort at all: it happens automatically when you open the site. No registration forms, no personal details. Copy your temporary address and start using it right away.

Fast

Your temporary mailbox is generated instantly. By the time the page has loaded, you already have an address and a password. Paste the address into a signup form, then receive the replies and read them right here on Temp Mail. No more registrations, form-filling and captcha-solving: your mailbox is always ready. No needless questions, no wasted clicks. Instant.

Intuitive

You won't run into layered menus or confusing settings here. We built the service so you stop losing time and patience the way you often do with the big mail providers. Unlike them, we're not trying to pull you into an ecosystem. Everything works the way you'd expect it to.

Anonymous

Temporary mail is dependable protection against spam, break-ins and inbox clutter. It's exactly what you want for signing up on dubious websites, forums, chat rooms and social networks. Your real mailbox stays out of harm's way, and advertising blasts and bot attacks become a thing of the past. Our temporary email service guarantees anonymity, zero commitments and full protection.

Flexible

The service suits beginners, who get a clear interface with no extra steps, and power users alike. The latter get API access and a quick way to register disposable mailboxes in bulk. That opens up plenty of room for testing apps and services, or for running multiple accounts in online games, social networks and elsewhere.

Smart

Using your permanent email everywhere online is like showing your passport to every passer-by. These days your email is effectively your digital passport, the master key to everything you own online, from payment services to cloud storage. It signs you into the Apple, Google and Microsoft ecosystems, and every critical action is confirmed through it. Whoever takes it over gains access to your entire digital life. That's why it makes sense to guard your main address and use a temporary one for dubious websites, forums, blogs, online shops and social networks.

Free

Every user gets full access to their temporary mailbox and an unlimited number of new ones. No hidden conditions, no paid extras: just a clean mailbox, its password, and no limit on how many disposable addresses you create. While the big mail providers charge for feature after feature, we give you convenience, reliability and privacy at no cost.

Everywhere

Temp Mail runs in the browser you already have: any device, any screen, nothing to download. Prefer it as an app? Your browser can put it on the home screen in one tap, with no app store, no permissions and no updates to chase. On a laptop at work or a phone on the train, the mailbox behaves exactly the same.
