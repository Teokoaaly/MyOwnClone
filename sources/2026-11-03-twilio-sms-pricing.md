## SMS/MMS Pricing

SMS pricing is based on the destination and type of message you're sending, as well as the carrier to which the SMS is being sent. Our pay-as-you-go pricing gives you control over your messaging costs, with discounts as you scale.

| Phone number type | SMS Outbound* | SMS Inbound* | MMS Outbound* | MMS Inbound* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Long codes A 10-digit number for SMS and voice calls with localized identity. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.022 | $0.0165 |
| Toll-free Business messaging phone number with a 3-digit prefix (Ex: 887) for high-throughput SMS and voice calls. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.022 | $0.02 |
| Short codes A short number (5-6 digits) designed for high-throughput two-way SMS messaging. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.022 | $0.0165 |

* Text messages are charged per segment. Learn more about character limits and message segments. Prices may change from time to time without notice and additional carrier fees may apply. See the carrier fees section below for more detail. US A2P 10DLC are subject to registration onboarding fees. A failed message processing fee of $0.001 per message will be applied only to messages that terminate in a "Failed" status.

RCS pricing is based on the destination and type of message you're sending, as well as the carrier to which the message is being sent. Our pay-as-you-go pricing gives you control over your messaging costs. No new API, no code changes, and all the reach of the Twilio platform.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RCS Rich Communication Services (RCS) enables you to send branded, interactive text messages. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.022 | $0.0165 |

* RCS Rich text messages are charged per segment and RCS Rich Media is charged per message. Prices may change from time to time without notice and additional carrier fees may apply. ## How does pricing differ for sending MMS compared to standard SMS?

SMS is billed per 160-character segment. MMS is billed at a flat rate that allows for media attachments (up to 5 MB) and up to 1,600 characters of text without triggering multi-segment billing. Outbound MMS in the US costs $0.022 per message across long codes, toll-free, and short codes. Inbound MMS costs $0.0165 per message for long codes and short codes, and $0.02 per message for toll-free numbers. Additional carrier fees may apply.

## How do US carrier passthrough fees and A2P 10DLC registration fees work?

US mobile carriers apply additional per-message carrier fees automatically based on the destination network (for example, AT&T charges $0.0035 for outbound SMS, while T-Mobile charges $0.0045). Additionally, US A2P 10DLC and RCS senders are subject to carrier registration onboarding fees. Twilio passes the fees through so you know exactly what you're paying, without increasing the price charged by the carriers.

## Does Twilio offer volume discounts for high-volume US SMS senders?

Yes. Twilio uses pay-as-you-go pricing that gives you control over messaging costs. Work with your Twilio account team to discuss volume discounts as your messaging volume scales.

## Are there additional costs for optional SMS features or failed messages?

Yes. Optional features are billed as add-ons: the Engagement Suite (link shortening, click tracking, and scheduling) costs $0.015 per outbound SMS (with the first 1,000 free monthly), and the Compliance Toolkit costs $0.015 per outbound SMS. A processing fee of $0.001 is applied per message that terminates in a "Failed" status.
