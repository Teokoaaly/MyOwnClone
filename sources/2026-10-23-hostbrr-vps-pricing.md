## Frequently Asked Questions

Do I get full root access?

Yes. Every VPS comes with full root/administrator access, allowing you to configure the server exactly as you need, install custom software, and modify kernels.


Virtual Private vs Virtual Dedicated?

Both utilize KVM virtualization for fully dedicated RAM and storage. A standard VPS shares CPU cores with fair usage. A VDS (Virtual Dedicated Server) provides fully dedicated CPU cores for 100% processing utilization, 24/7.


Can I upgrade my VPS later?

Yes, you can easily scale up to a larger package right from your client area. Resizing simply requires a quick reboot to recognize the new resources.
