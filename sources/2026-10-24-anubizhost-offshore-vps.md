## What Is Offshore Hosting?

Offshore hosting means your server is located in a jurisdiction with strong privacy laws and limited international cooperation on content takedowns. This provides an extra layer of legal protection for your data and operations.

Our offshore infrastructure spans multiple continents, giving you the freedom to choose the jurisdiction that best fits your privacy and compliance requirements.
