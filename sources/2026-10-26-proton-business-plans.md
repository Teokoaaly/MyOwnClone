## Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between Workspace Standard and Workspace Premium?

View details

Workspace Standard and Workspace Premium both give you Proton’s core suite: Proton Mail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, Meet, VPN, and Pass. The Standard tier provides each user with 1 TB of encrypted storage, supports up to 15 custom email domains, and allows video meetings for up to 100 participants at once. The Premium tier expands those limits: each user receives 3 TB of storage, you can configure up to 20 custom email domains, and video meetings can accommodate up to 250 participants simultaneously. In addition, Premium subscribers gain access to Lumo, Proton’s privacy‑first chat assistant.

How does end-to-end encryption protect my business data?

View details

[End-to-end encryption](https://proton.me/security/end-to-end-encryption) protects your business data by making sure that only the people you choose can access the information being shared. The data is encrypted on your device and stays encrypted while it’s transmitted or stored, only being decrypted when it reaches the intended recipient.

For businesses, an end-to-end encrypted team collaboration tools help safeguard sensitive information, reduce the risk of data breaches, and support compliance with data protection regulations. It's a key tool in maintaining privacy, trust, and control over your data.

How can I migrate my company’s data to Proton?

View details

Our [help guides](https://proton.me/support/how-to-get-your-business-started-in-proton-mail) make it easy to switch to Proton from an existing virtual workspace. What is a user?

View details

Users are accounts in your organization that aren’t administrators. Users can log in to their own accounts for Mail, Calendar, Drive, Pass, and VPN using their email address and password. They can’t view or manage any details at the organization level. You can add or remove users, as well as allocate storage and VPN connections, from your admin panel.

[Learn more about managing users.](https://proton.me/support/add-users-organization)

What if I reach my plan’s storage limit?

View details

If you need more storage space, simply add more users and reallocate that storage or upgrade to a plan that offers more storage. For large organizations, you can customize your storage with a Proton Enterprise plan by [contacting our Sales team](https://proton.me/business/contact).
