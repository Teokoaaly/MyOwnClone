## Plans & pricing FAQs

How does the Multilogin pricing work?

Multilogin offers a free plan and paid subscriptions based on the number of profiles and features you need.

- The free plan has no time limit and requires no credit card.
- Paid plans start from **$7.08/month**.
- You can choose your profile limit and upgrade as your needs grow.
- Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing.
- Downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle.

All plans combine cloud mobile phones, browser profiles, built-in proxies and mobile minutes in one platform.

What changed in Multilogin pricing in July 2026?

In July 2026, Multilogin introduced a **free plan** with no time limit and no credit card required, making it easier to explore the platform before upgrading.

The free plan includes 5 cloud mobile and browser profiles, 200 MB of premium proxy traffic, and one cloud mobile profile with 30 minutes of use.

Paid plans now start from $7.08 per month when billed annually and include higher profile limits, monthly proxy traffic, mobile minutes, automation features, and additional tools for larger teams.

How do Multilogin subscription upgrades and downgrades work?

Monthly subscriptions can be upgraded anytime with prorated billing. Annual subscriptions include a 35% discount compared to monthly pricing. When upgrading yearly plans, the remaining value of your subscription is prorated toward the new plan. If the upgraded plan costs less than what you already paid, the difference is returned as Multipoints credit. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What is a cloud phone?

[A cloud phone](https://multilogin.com/mobile/cloud-phone/) is a fully functional Android device hosted in the cloud. It runs on real smartphone hardware, but you don’t need to own the device.

With Multilogin Cloud Phones, you get secure, isolated Android environments for managing multiple social media accounts, apps, and workflows from a single dashboard.

You can start with Multilogin’s free plan, which includes one cloud mobile profile with 30 minutes of use.

Is Multilogin an Android emulator?

No. Multilogin is not an Android emulator. It provides cloud phones running Android on real smartphone hardware.

Unlike emulators, which simulate Android on a computer, Multilogin Cloud Phones provide isolated mobile environments with unique device profiles. You can run Android apps and manage multiple accounts without buying or maintaining physical phones.

How are cloud phones billed in Multilogin?

Cloud phones are billed based on usage time. Your plan includes a set number of mobile minutes, and the timer runs only while the cloud phone is active.

The timer starts when the cloud phone launches and stops when you select **Stop** in the dashboard. Once your included minutes are used up, you can purchase additional mobile minutes as needed, with prices starting at **$0.0073 per minute**.

The free plan includes **30 mobile minutes as a one-time bonus**, while paid plans include a monthly allowance based on the selected plan.

Is there a limit on how long a cloud phone can run?

A session can run as long as you need, and Multilogin bills only the **first 180 minutes** of one uninterrupted session. Any runtime after that is free. If auto-stop is on, the profile stops at the time you set.

Will I lose my minutes if I forget to stop a cloud phone?

At most 180 minutes per session, because Multilogin stops billing there. To lose even less, **turn on auto-stop** and choose a shorter timer. If you stop the profile manually, you pay only for the time used.

What subscription plans does Multilogin offer?

Multilogin offers a **Free, Pro, and Business plan**, with all prices in USD.

**Free plan:** Includes 5 cloud mobile and browser profiles, a one-time bonus of 200 MB of premium proxy traffic, and 1 cloud mobile profile with 30 minutes of use. There is **no time limit and no credit card required**.

**Pro plans:** Choose between 10, 20, or 50 profiles. Pro plans include monthly premium proxy traffic and mobile minutes, team seats, and automation-friendly API access.

**Business plans:** Start at 100 profiles and are designed for larger teams and agencies. They include unlimited team seats, advanced team management, and custom API rate limits, with premium proxy traffic and mobile minutes growing with the plan — from 5 GB and 150 mobile minutes per month on Business 100.

Paid plans start from **$7.08/month**.

Does Multilogin offer a free plan?

**Yes.** Multilogin offers a **free plan with no time limit and no credit card required**, so you can explore the platform before upgrading.

The Free plan includes:

- 5 cloud mobile and browser profiles
- 200 MB of premium proxy traffic (one-time bonus)
- 1 cloud profile with 30 mobile minutes (one-time bonus)

When you need more profiles, additional monthly mobile minutes, premium proxy traffic, or advanced features such as API access and team management, you can upgrade to a paid plan at any time.

What are the advantages of Multilogin?

Multilogin brings cloud mobile and browser profile management together in one platform, helping users manage multiple accounts without relying on multiple physical devices.

Key advantages include secure, isolated profiles; persistent app data and login sessions; built-in premium proxies; and tools for cloning profiles, bulk actions, API automation, and team collaboration.

Multilogin also supports both mobile-first and browser-first accounts from a single dashboard, making it easier to scale social media, agency, e-commerce, and other multi-account workflows. Users can start with the free plan and upgrade as their profile, proxy, mobile-minute, or automation needs grow.

Does Multilogin offer a referral program?

Yes! [Multilogin offers a referral program](https://multilogin.com/referral-program/)

for users on paid plans. Share your referral link or code and earn **15% from eligible plan payments and renewals** made by your referrals.

Rewards become available after a 45-day waiting period and can be used toward Multilogin plans, mobile minutes, or proxy traffic. Referral rewards cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred to another account.

You continue earning from successful renewals for as long as the referred user’s subscription remains active.

Which Android versions are available in Multilogin for cloud phones?

Multilogin Cloud Phones support **Android versions 10 through 16**. You can choose the Android version when creating a cloud mobile profile based on the apps and workflow you need.

Android 14–16 are recommended for modern apps, while older versions are available when specific app compatibility is required.

Which Multilogin plans include access to the automation token?

Automation-friendly API access is included with all **Pro and Business plans**, starting from **Pro 10**.

The Free plan does not include API access.

Is Multilogin safe to use?

Yes. **Multilogin** is designed with security and privacy in mind. Each cloud phone and each browser profile runs in its own **fully isolated environment**, so one profile never crosses over into another. This keeps your accounts separate and protects you from unwanted account linking.

Cloud phones use real Android hardware identifiers, and browser profiles get their own cookies, session, and IP, so every profile behaves like a genuine, independent device or browser.

Multilogin is fully GDPR-compliant and supports two-factor authentication (2FA) to protect your account. You can store profile data locally or in secure cloud storage for full flexibility (on the Free plan, cloud storage is the only available option).

Together, this makes Multilogin a safe foundation for managing multiple social profiles and collaborating with a team.
