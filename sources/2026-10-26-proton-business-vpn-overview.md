## Frequently asked questions

What does a business VPN do?

View details

A business VPN (virtual private network) creates a secure, encrypted connection between your company’s devices and its network. This allows employees to safely access company data and resources from remote locations without exposing sensitive information to cyber threats. It essentially acts as a private tunnel for data to travel through, protecting it from hackers, surveillance, and other risks associated with public internet connections. A business VPN can also help employees access geo-restricted services, bypass censorship, and improve overall security across the network.

What VPN should I use for my business?

View details

When choosing a business VPN, look for features like strong encryption, granular access control, activity monitoring, and a vast range of server locations.

Proton VPN for Business offers all of these alongside additional advanced controls designed to give you full control over your organization’s network.

Does my small business need a VPN?

View details

If your business handles sensitive customer data, has remote employees, or employees that use public WiFi, a VPN can significantly enhance your security and privacy. A business VPN helps to prevent data breaches, secure online communications, and ensure safe access to company resources. Even if your business is small, the cost of a VPN is generally outweighed by the potential risks of cyber attacks or data leaks.
