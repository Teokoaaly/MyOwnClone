## Choose the right server for your needs:

###### VPS (Shared vCPU)

Shares physical resources with other virtual servers on the same physical machine. You are allocated a specific portion of CPU (cores), RAM, and disk space. However, access to these resources may be limited or affected by other users on the server, unlike with a VDS.


###### VDS (Dedicated vCPU)

Provides fully dedicated resources such as CPU cores, RAM, and disk space. This guarantees consistent performance and ensures your server operates independently from others on the same physical host.


###### VDS with GPU

Each VPS comes with a fully dedicated GPU — one physical GPU per virtual server, without sharing. This guarantees consistent GPU performance for compute-intensive workloads such as ML, rendering, or video processing. CPU and RAM resources, however, are shared among all VPS on the host, allowing for efficient resource utilization while keeping costs reasonable.

## FAQ

## What is Bitcoin VPS hosting?

Bitcoin VPS hosting is a virtual server rental paid for with cryptocurrency, such as Bitcoin or other digital assets. This solution allows you to deploy infrastructure in a secure and isolated environment for any online project.


## How do I buy a VPS with Bitcoin?

To sign up for a VPS with HOSTKEY, simply select the desired configuration, proceed to payment, and specify cryptocurrency as the payment method. After the transaction is successfully confirmed, the server is created automatically.


## Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

HOSTKEY accepts not only Bitcoin but also a number of other popular digital currencies, including Ethereum and Litecoin. The specific list of available coins may depend on the payment system used.


## Do I need to verify my identity to pay with crypto?

In most cases, cryptocurrency payments are processed without standard bank identity verification. However, individual payment providers may request additional information according to their requirements.


## How can I access my Bitcoin VPS?

Connection to a HOSTKEY VPS is via SSH for Linux servers or RDP for Windows. This provides full administrative access to the system and its settings.


## Can I host websites and applications on a Bitcoin VPS?

Yes, HOSTKEY's VPS is suitable for hosting websites, applications, APIs, and other digital services. Capabilities are limited only by the selected server resources.


## Is Bitcoin VPS hosting secure?

Each virtual server operates in an isolated environment and is protected by basic security measures, including a firewall and DDoS protection. HOSTKEY also allows you to customize the level of protection to suit your needs.


## Why is my crypto payment marked as unpaid?

Cryptocurrency payments require blockchain confirmation, so processing may take some time. If the status isn't updated, please wait for network confirmations or contact HOSTKEY support.
