## SendGrid Email API FAQs

### How does Twilio SendGrid Email API pricing work?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Twilio SendGrid pricing comes down to two things: how much email you send each month and which features you need. Plans run from a free 60-day trial to Essentials at $19.95/mo, Pro at $89.95/mo, and custom Premier pricing. Volume discounts kick in as you scale.

### What's the difference between the Essentials, Pro, and Premier plans?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Each tier adds more as your program grows:

- Essentials: Core sending, tracking, and deliverability tools for smaller programs

- Pro: Dedicated IPs, subuser management, single sign-on, and email testing

- Premier: Custom volume, more email validations, and expert program management


### Which Twilio SendGrid Email API plan is right for me?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Start with your monthly send volume. Testing or sending a trickle? The free trial gets you going. Sending 50,000 to 100,000 a month? Essentials fits. Scaling into the millions? Pro adds dedicated IPs and SSO. High volume with custom needs? That's Premier.

### How does the free trial work?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Twilio SendGrid's free trial runs $0 for 60 days and lets you send 100 emails a day — no credit card, no commitment. You get analytics, deliverability optimization, and the dynamic template editor, so you can test the real platform before committing to a paid plan.

### What happens if I go over my plan's email limit?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Send past what your plan includes and overage charges may apply on top of your monthly rate. If you're regularly pushing your limit, upgrading to the next tier usually beats paying overages. Check your plan details for the specifics.

### Can I upgrade my plan as I grow?

An icon of a plus symbolAn icon of a minus symbol

Yes. You can upgrade any time after you set you your free account. Twilio SendGrid plans are built to scale with you. Move from Essentials to Pro to Premier as your volume climbs and your feature needs grow, without getting locking into more than you need today.
