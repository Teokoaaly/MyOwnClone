## Frequently askedquestions

### How much does Quackr cost?

Free numbers cost nothing. An app number, a private number for one app, starts at $4.10 a month. A private number for every app and website starts at $14.99 a month, or $39.99 for 12 months. Enterprise customers get a custom quote for bulk numbers. All prices are in US dollars.

### How much does a private number cost?

It depends on the country and how long you rent it for. The cards above always show today's lowest price, and longer rentals cost less per day.

### What's the difference between a free number and a private number?

Free numbers are shared with everyone, so anyone can read their messages and many apps block them. A private number is yours alone for the whole rental. Only you see its messages, and it's a real non-VoIP number that works with virtually any platform.

### What is an app number?

A private number for one app you choose, such as WhatsApp, Telegram or Google. It's yours alone for that app, it costs less than a number for every app, and you can rent it for 1 month to 1 year in the US, UK or Australia.

### Can I extend my rental?

Yes. You can extend at any time before it expires and keep the same number and messages. We'll remind you before it ends.

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard and American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Bitcoin, Ethereum and the other major cryptocurrencies.

### Can I send texts from my number?

No. Quackr numbers receive SMS only, which keeps them reliable and the price low.

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, if the number doesn't work as described and you haven't used it. Our refund policy has the details.

### Do you offer bulk or business pricing?

Yes. We give large discounts on monthly rentals, depending on how many numbers you need and for how long. Tell us what you need and we'll reply with a custom quote.
