## Get answers to common questions

Find everything you need to know about Proxifly pricing and plans

## What counts as an API request?

Each call to the Proxifly API counts as one request. This includes fetching a single proxy or a batch of proxies. Checking your account status or usage does not count toward your limit.

## What types of proxies do you offer?

We offer HTTP, HTTPS, SOCKS4, and SOCKS5 proxies. All proxies are tested and verified before being served. Higher-tier plans unlock access to all protocol types.

## Can I filter proxies by country?

Yes! You can filter by country code in your API request. The Free plan supports 10 countries, Starter supports 50, and Pro/Enterprise plans give you access to all 100+ countries.

## How does auto rotation work?

With auto rotation enabled, each API request returns a different proxy from our pool. This helps avoid rate limiting and IP bans. Available on Starter plans and above.

## Can I cancel or change plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No long-term contracts required.

## Do unused requests roll over?

No, request quotas reset at the beginning of each billing cycle. If you consistently need more requests, we recommend upgrading to a plan that better fits your usage.

## Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes! All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You get full access to all features during the trial period.

## What are dedicated proxies?

Dedicated proxies on the Enterprise plan are private IPs reserved exclusively for your account. They offer the highest reliability and are never shared with other users.
