Common questions about buying VPS with Bitcoin and cryptocurrency

### Which cryptocurrencies do you accept?

We accept 50+ cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Litecoin (LTC), Bitcoin Cash (BCH), Dogecoin (DOGE), Ripple (XRP), Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC), Binance Coin (BNB), Cardano (ADA), Solana (SOL), Polygon (MATIC), Monero (XMR), and many more. ### Is KYC or identity verification required?

No identity verification is required when paying with cryptocurrency. You only need to provide an email address to create your account. We respect your privacy and don't require passport, ID, address verification, or any KYC documentation for crypto payments.

### How long does crypto payment take to process?

Cryptocurrency payments are processed automatically after blockchain confirmation. Bitcoin typically confirms in 10-30 minutes, Ethereum in 2-5 minutes, and some altcoins even faster. Your VPS is deployed instantly after payment confirmation.

### Are there any fees for paying with crypto?

We don't charge any surcharge for cryptocurrency payments - prices are identical to credit card pricing. You will need to pay standard blockchain network fees (gas fees) when sending your payment, which vary by cryptocurrency and network congestion.

### Can I get a refund if I paid with cryptocurrency?

Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds for cryptocurrency payments due to the irreversible nature of blockchain transactions and exchange rate volatility. This policy is stated in our terms of service. We recommend testing with our 5-day money-back guarantee using traditional payment methods first if you're unsure.

### How do I pay for VPS with Bitcoin?

During checkout, select cryptocurrency as your payment method. You'll be redirected to our crypto payment processor where you can choose Bitcoin or another cryptocurrency. You'll receive a payment address and amount to send. Once your transaction confirms on the blockchain, your VPS is automatically activated.

### Do you keep logs of crypto payments?

We maintain transaction records for accounting purposes showing the payment amount and date, but we don't link these to wallet addresses or track blockchain transactions. Our payment processor handles crypto payments, and we only receive confirmation of successful payment.

### Can I use a privacy coin like Monero?

Yes, we accept Monero (XMR) and other privacy-focused cryptocurrencies. Privacy coins offer enhanced privacy through features like stealth addresses and ring signatures, making them ideal for users who prioritize not sharing transaction details.

### What happens if I send the wrong amount?

If you send less than the required amount, your payment will be pending until you send the difference. If you overpay, the excess amount will typically be credited to your account balance. Contact support if you need assistance with a cryptocurrency payment issue.

### Can I set up automatic crypto payments?

Currently, cryptocurrency renewals are manual - you'll receive an invoice before renewal and can pay with crypto again. We're working on implementing automatic cryptocurrency billing, but due to blockchain transaction complexity, this requires manual payment for now. You can also add account credit in advance.
