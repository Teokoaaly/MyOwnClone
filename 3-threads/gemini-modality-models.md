# Gemini Modality Models
*Last updated: 2026-11-01*

## Overview
Beyond core text models, Gemini extends across multiple modalities — embeddings, image generation, and music — each with distinct pricing structures, capability profiles, and use-case boundaries. These modality-specific models share the same underlying Google infrastructure but serve fundamentally different workflows: retrieval and similarity search for embeddings, creative generation for images, and per-request song composition for music. The modality expansion means that "Gemini" now encompasses a family of specialized APIs rather than a single model interface, requiring developers to understand the pricing and friction profile of each lane.

## Key Ideas
Gemini Embedding 2 is the first multimodal embedding model from Google, mapping text, images, video, audio, and PDFs into a unified embedding space where different modalities can be compared directly. Pricing is modular: $0.20 per 1M text tokens, $0.45 per 1M image inputs ($0.00012 per image), $6.50 per 1M audio inputs ($0.00016 per second), and $12.00 per 1M video inputs ($0.00079 per frame). The unified embedding space enables cross-modal retrieval — searching images with text queries, finding relevant audio clips, or clustering PDF documents by content — but the per-modality pricing makes it cost-effective for batch image embedding and audio feature extraction rather than high-frequency use cases. Gemini image generation models (3.1 Flash Image at $0.50 text/image input, $3.00 output including thinking; 3.1 Flash Lite Image at $0.25 input, $1.50 output) price per-image rather than per-token, with 1K (1024x1024px) consuming 1120 tokens at $0.045 per 1K. These models support grounding with Google Web and Image Search (5K free monthly, then $14/1K) but lack context caching. Lyria music models price per request/song rather than per token: $0.08 per song for Lyria 3.5 full songs, $0.04 per 30s clip preview. Per-song pricing creates cost predictability for complete compositions but introduces uncertainty for longer works, as a 5-minute song and a 2-minute song both count as one song at $0.08 despite differing token consumption and generation compute costs. No context caching or grounding features are available for music, limiting use to standalone generation rather than retrieval-augmented composition. All three atoms in this cluster ([[2026-11-01-gemini-embedding-2]], [[2026-11-01-gemini-image-models]], [[2026-11-01-gemini-music-models]]) represent non-overlapping modality lanes, meaning that cost optimization and use-case mapping are independent per modality — a team building RAG pipelines evaluates Embedding 2, a creative team evaluates Image models, and a media team evaluates Lyria, with little cross-modality friction beyond shared Google Search grounding if grounding is needed.

## Open Questions
- Does the unified embedding space in Embedding 2 actually enable effective cross-modal similarity, or do modality-specific embedding dimensions produce misleading similarity scores?
- For image generation teams needing grounding, what is the practical cost of the 5K free Google Search monthly quota when combined with other Gemini 3+ model usage in the same organization?
- Can Lyria's per-song pricing model be extended to multi-part compositions or album-length works without losing the cost-predictability advantage?
- Will Google introduce context caching or grounding features for image or music models, and how would that affect the pricing friction profile of each modality?
- For teams starting with Embedding 2, what is the typical migration path when cross-modal retrieval needs expand beyond the unified space's capabilities?
